About Knexio

Knexio is a growing collection of free browser-based tools built for writers, students, SEO professionals, and everyday users. No registration, no installation, no hidden fees — just fast, accurate tools that work directly in your browser. From word counters and reading time calculators to JSON formatters and QR code generators, every tool is designed with one goal in mind: save you time and help you work smarter.

In addition to our core tools, Knexio also features a curated library of step-by-step troubleshooting guides covering popular apps and software, a selection of free mini-games for quick breaks and mental exercise, and a business tools suite for professionals — all accessible without an account. Whether you need to analyze text, convert file formats, calculate finance figures, or simply pass the time with a quick puzzle, Knexio has something for you.
