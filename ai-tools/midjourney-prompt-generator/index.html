How It Works

Our Midjourney prompt generator combines essential visual elements to create comprehensive prompts. Start with your subject—the main focus of your image. Be descriptive and specific to guide the AI's interpretation. Choose an art style that matches your vision: photorealistic for lifelike images, digital art for modern graphics, oil painting for classic artistry, watercolor for soft aesthetics, anime style for Japanese animation, 3D render for dimensional depth, or pencil sketch for hand-drawn textures.

Select lighting to set the mood and atmosphere. Golden hour creates warm, nostalgic tones; soft natural light provides even illumination; dramatic lighting adds contrast and intensity; neon lights bring vibrant energy; moonlight creates mysterious nighttime scenes; studio lighting offers controlled professional results. Composition determines how elements are arranged in the frame—centered for focus, rule of thirds for balanced interest, wide angle for expansive scenes, close-up for detail, bird's eye view for overhead perspective, or low angle for dramatic impact.

Add any additional details to refine your vision—colors, textures, emotions, or atmospheric elements. Click generate and copy your prompt directly to Midjourney. The prompt includes the standard Midjourney command format with aspect ratio (--ar 16:9) and version (--v 6) parameters for optimal results.
