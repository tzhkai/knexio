How It Works

Paste or type the text you want to summarize into the input area. This can be an article, research paper, report, email thread, or any long-form text. The tool accepts text of any length and works with various types of content including academic writing, news articles, business documents, and more.

Select your preferred summary length from the dropdown menu. Choose "Short" for a very brief overview, "Medium" for a balanced summary, or "Long" for more detailed coverage of key points. The length option determines how much information is retained in the summary.

Click "Summarize" to process your text. The AI analyzes the content, identifies the most important sentences and key information, and generates a coherent summary. Review the generated summary and copy it to use in your notes, reports, or presentations.

An AI text summarizer uses artificial intelligence to analyze long texts and automatically generate concise summaries that capture the main points and key information.
