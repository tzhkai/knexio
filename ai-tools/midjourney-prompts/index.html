Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Midjourney prompt?

A Midjourney prompt is a text description that guides the AI to create a specific image. Effective prompts include subject details, art style, lighting, mood, and composition instructions to achieve desired results.

How do I create good Midjourney prompts?

Be specific about your subject, include art style keywords (photorealistic, digital art, oil painting), describe lighting conditions (golden hour, neon lights), and mention composition techniques (centered, rule of thirds) for better results.

Can I use these prompts for other AI art tools?

Yes. While optimized for Midjourney, these prompts work well with other AI image generators like DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, and Leonardo AI with minor adjustments.

What parameters does this tool add?

The tool automatically adds recommended parameters like aspect ratio (--ar), version number (--v), and quality settings based on your description to help you get consistent results.

Is my prompt data stored or shared?

No. All prompt generation happens locally in your browser. Your descriptions and generated prompts are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties.
