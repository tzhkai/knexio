## What this means

There is no evidence yet that the new site has failed to attract traffic; there is simply not yet a post-launch performance dataset to interpret. The next reliable checkpoint is when Search Console’s performance report includes 15 August and later. Until then, use URL Inspection, sitemap status, crawl requests, and the known indexed workflow URLs as the meaningful early indicators.
