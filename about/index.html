About Knexio

Why We Built This

Knexio started with a frustration we all know: you need a quick word count, a JSON formatter, or a unit conversion, and every result on Google wants you to sign up, install something, or pay. Over the years, we built dozens of tiny utilities for our own workflow — tools we used daily and kept refining because they actually solved problems.

In early 2025, we decided to put them all in one place and open them to everyone. No sign-up walls. No premium tiers. No data collection. Just fast, functional tools that work the moment you land on the page.

How We Work

Every tool on Knexio runs entirely in your browser. When you use our word counter, the text never leaves your device. When you format JSON or generate a QR code, the computation happens locally. We do not store or log anything you type — and since there's no server to process it, there's nothing to hand over.

We fund the site through advertising, which is why you'll see a few ad placements around the tools — but never inside the tool workspace itself. The tool you're using is always ad-free.
