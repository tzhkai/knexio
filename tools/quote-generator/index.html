How many quotes are available?

Our collection includes hundreds of curated quotes from famous thinkers, leaders, and authors across categories including Motivational, Inspirational, Success, Life, Wisdom, and Business.

Can I browse by category or author?

Yes. Use the category filter to browse quotes by theme (Motivational, Success, Life, etc.). Select an author to see quotes attributed to that person.

Can I share quotes on social media?

Yes. Click "Share on Twitter" to open a tweet with the current quote pre-filled. You can also copy any quote and paste it into any social platform.

Are quotes randomly selected?

Each click generates a random quote from the selected category. If no category is selected, the quote is drawn from the entire collection.
