Free Online Calorie Calculator

Calculate your daily calorie needs based on age, weight, height, gender, and activity level. Get your BMR, TDEE, and personalized recommendations for weight goals.

How It Works

The calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to compute Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), then applies activity level factors to determine Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). Results include BMI, suggested calorie targets for weight loss/gain, and macronutrient breakdown (carbs, protein, fat). Supports metric (kg/cm) and imperial (lbs/ft) units.
