Frequently Asked Questions

What are meta tags?

Meta tags are HTML snippets that describe a page's content. They don't appear on the visible page but provide information to search engines and social media platforms about your page's topic, description, and appearance when shared.

Why are meta tags important for SEO?

Meta tags help search engines understand your page content and display it correctly in search results. Well-optimized title and description tags can improve click-through rates, while Open Graph tags ensure your content looks attractive when shared on social media.

What is the ideal length for meta titles and descriptions?

Meta titles should be 50-60 characters for optimal display in search results. Meta descriptions should be 150-160 characters to ensure the full description appears without being truncated. This tool includes character limits to help you stay within these guidelines.

What are Open Graph tags?

Open Graph tags are meta tags that control how your content appears when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, and other social platforms. They define the title, description, image, and URL that appear in social media previews, making your shared content more engaging and clickable.

What are Twitter Cards?

Twitter Cards are specialized meta tags that enable rich media attachments in tweets. They allow you to add images, videos, and other media to your tweets when your content is shared, increasing engagement and click-through rates.

What is a canonical link?

A canonical link tells search engines which version of a page should be indexed when multiple versions exist (e.g., HTTP vs HTTPS, or with/without www). This prevents duplicate content issues and ensures SEO authority is consolidated to your preferred URL.
