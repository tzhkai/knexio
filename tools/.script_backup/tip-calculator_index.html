What Is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you quickly work out how much to tip at a restaurant, split the bill among friends, and see the total amount per person.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard tip?

15–20% is customary in the US. In other countries, tipping norms vary — always check local customs.
