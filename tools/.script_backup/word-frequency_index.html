What Is a Word Frequency Counter?

A word frequency counter analyses a block of text and shows how often each word appears, sorted by frequency — useful for SEO keyword analysis, writing style checks, and text research.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it ignore common words like "the" and "a"?

Yes. Toggle the "Ignore stop words" option to filter out common English function words.
