What Is a Roman Numeral Converter?

A Roman numeral converter translates between standard Arabic numbers (1–3999) and Roman numerals (I, V, X, L, C, D, M) — useful for clocks, outlines, and historical documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the largest Roman numeral?

MMMCMXCIX = 3,999. Numbers above 3,999 require an overline notation not supported in standard text.
