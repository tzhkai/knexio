What Is a BMI Calculator?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple measure of body fat based on height and weight. It helps assess whether you are underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI always accurate?

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure. Athletes may have a high BMI due to muscle mass rather than fat.
