What Is a Word Wrap Tool?

A word wrap tool automatically inserts line breaks into long lines of text at a specified column width — useful for formatting code, emails, and terminal output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does word wrap break words mid-character?

No. It wraps at word boundaries to keep words intact.
