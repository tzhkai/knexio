What Is a Calorie Calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates your daily caloric needs based on age, gender, height, weight, and activity level using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

Frequently Asked Questions

A deficit of 500 calories per day typically leads to about 0.5 kg (1 lb) of weight loss per week.
