What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a writing utility that measures the length and structure of a text. It helps writers, students, and professionals track word limits, sentence counts, and paragraph structure when creating essays, articles, academic papers, or SEO content.

Who Should Use This Tool?

This word counter is designed for students working on essays, writers editing articles, marketers preparing SEO content, and anyone who needs accurate word and character counts.
