What is a Sentence Length Checker?

A sentence length checker measures how long your sentences are by calculating the average number of words per sentence. It is commonly used to improve readability, academic writing quality, and content clarity.

Why Does Sentence Length Matter?

Sentence length affects readability and comprehension. Short sentences improve clarity, while overly long sentences may reduce engagement. Academic and ESL writing often follow recommended average sentence lengths of 15–20 words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sentence length checker?

What is the ideal average sentence length?

For most writing, an average of 15 to 20 words per sentence is considered clear and readable. Academic writing may use slightly longer sentences.
