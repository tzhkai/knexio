What Is a Slug Generator?

A slug generator converts a title or phrase into a URL-friendly slug by lowercasing text, replacing spaces with hyphens, and removing special characters — essential for SEO-friendly URLs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are hyphens preferred over underscores in URLs?

Google treats hyphens as word separators, which improves keyword recognition and SEO.
