About Scientific Calculator

This free online scientific calculator supports trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan), logarithms (log, ln), exponents, factorials, and more. Switch between DEG and RAD modes for angle calculations.

Keyboard shortcuts: 0-9 for numbers, +-*/ for operators, Enter or = to calculate, Escape to clear, Backspace to delete.
