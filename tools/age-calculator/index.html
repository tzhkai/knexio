How to Use the Age Calculator

Select your date of birth using the date picker and click "Calculate Age". Your exact age is displayed instantly in years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

How It Works

The calculator computes the exact difference between your birth date and today's date, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths. It displays age in multiple units and calculates your next birthday countdown and zodiac sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the age calculator?

The calculator uses JavaScript's Date API to compute the exact difference between your birth date and today. It accounts for leap years and varying month lengths, giving you a precise result down to the second.

Does the calculator consider leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses the actual calendar including leap years when computing total days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

What zodiac sign does the calculator show?

The calculator uses the standard tropical zodiac system. It shows the zodiac sign for your birth date based on the month and day of birth.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

No. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser. Your date of birth never leaves your device and is not saved on any server.

Can I calculate age for future dates?

Yes. Simply enter any date in the past or future, and the calculator will show the time difference.
