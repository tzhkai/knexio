Frequently Asked Questions

What is my maximum heart rate?

Max HR = 220 minus your age. For a 30-year-old, max is 190 bpm.

Which zone is best for fat loss?

Zone 2 (60-70% of max) is optimal for burning fat through sustained aerobic exercise.

Should I exercise in the maximum zone?

Only for short bursts. Zone 5 is for athletes doing high-intensity interval training.
