Free Online Sentence Counter for Essays

This sentence counter instantly calculates sentences, words, and characters in academic essays. Perfect for students, researchers, and educators who need to meet assignment requirements.

Use it to ensure your essays meet minimum sentence limits, improve readability, and structure your academic writing effectively.

How It Works

The tool processes your text in real time, splitting sentences by punctuation marks (periods, question marks, exclamation points). It also counts words and characters to provide comprehensive statistics for your essay.
