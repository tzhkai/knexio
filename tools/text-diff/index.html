Free Online Text Diff Checker

This text diff checker instantly compares two versions of any text and highlights the differences. Additions are shown in green, deletions in red, making it easy to spot exactly what changed.

Perfect for writers comparing drafts, developers reviewing code changes, or anyone tracking revisions between document versions.

How It Works

The tool compares the two texts word by word using a longest common subsequence algorithm. It then highlights additions in green and deletions in red, showing you exactly which words were added, removed, or changed.

All comparison happens entirely in your browser — your text is never sent to any server.

Compare AI Outputs Side by Side

When you ask an AI model to rewrite or improve a piece of text, it can be hard to spot what actually changed. Paste the original response in the left box and the revised version in the right box, then click Compare. The diff view shows you precisely which words were added, removed, or replaced — making it easy to evaluate whether the AI's edits are improvements.
