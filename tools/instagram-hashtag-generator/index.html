How It Works

This Instagram hashtag generator analyzes your topic or keyword to find matching categories from our curated collection of popular hashtags across various niches. If your topic matches a category (like "fitness", "travel", "food"), it uses those specific hashtags. Otherwise, it creates custom hashtags based on your keyword and adds popular general hashtags.

The tool randomly shuffles the combined hashtag set and selects your requested number of hashtags (10-30). You can regenerate different combinations by clicking "Regenerate" until you find the perfect mix for your post. Simply click "Copy All" to copy the hashtags and paste them directly into your Instagram post.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?

Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post. While some users use all 30, studies show that 9-15 hashtags often provide the best engagement. Use this tool to generate the optimal number for your strategy and experiment with different counts.

What makes a good hashtag strategy?

A good hashtag strategy combines three types: niche-specific hashtags (targeting your exact audience), popular trending hashtags (for broader reach), and branded hashtags (for your unique identity). This tool helps you find relevant hashtags from curated categories and generate custom ones.

Should I put hashtags in comments or captions?

Both methods work! Many users place hashtags in the first comment to keep captions clean and readable. Others include them directly in the caption. Instagram treats both the same, so choose whichever style fits your aesthetic preference.

Can I use the same hashtags repeatedly?

Yes, but Instagram's algorithm may reduce reach for repeated hashtag sets. It's better to rotate hashtags, use this tool to generate fresh combinations, and tailor hashtags to each post's specific content for optimal engagement.

What topics are supported?

The generator supports popular categories including fitness, travel, food, photography, fashion, nature, business, and technology. If your topic doesn't match these categories, the tool creates custom hashtags based on your keyword and adds popular general hashtags.

Are these hashtags optimized for Instagram's algorithm?

The hashtags are curated from popular, frequently-used sets across various niches. While we can't guarantee algorithm performance, using relevant, mixed-combination hashtags (niche + general) is a proven strategy for maximizing reach and engagement on Instagram.
