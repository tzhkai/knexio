How to Use

What Is Text to Speech?

A text-to-speech tool converts written text into spoken audio using your browser's built-in speech synthesis engine — useful for proofreading, accessibility, and language learning.

This tool works entirely in your browser, so your text is never sent to any server.

How It Works - Enter or paste text in the input area, select voice and speed, then click Convert. Listen instantly in your browser or download as audio file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What voices are available?

Browser-based voices vary by system. You can choose from available system voices in different languages.

Can I download the audio?

Yes, download the generated speech as an MP3 file.
