What Is an Online Sentence Counter?

This online sentence counter helps you instantly measure how many sentences your text contains. It works in real time as you type or paste, requiring no button presses or page reloads.

How to Use

Step 1: Paste or type your text into the input area above.

Step 2: View real-time sentence, word, and character counts below.

Step 3: Use the Clear button to reset or Copy to save your text.

How It Works - Paste or type your text and get instant sentence, word, and character counts. The tool automatically detects sentence boundaries and handles various punctuation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are sentences detected?

Sentences are detected by periods, exclamation marks, and question marks followed by space or end of text.
