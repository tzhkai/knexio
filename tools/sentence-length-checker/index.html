What Is a Sentence Length Checker?

This sentence length checker analyzes your text and calculates the average words per sentence. It also provides a readability assessment to help you understand how easy or difficult your writing is to read.

How to Use

Step 1: Paste or type your text into the input area above.

Step 2: View real-time sentence count, word count, and average sentence length.

Step 3: Check the readability tip for advice on improving your writing style.

How It Works - Paste your text and get instant analysis of sentence lengths. See average sentence length, longest sentences, and distribution. Helpful for improving writing clarity and meeting readability requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the ideal sentence length?

Most writing guides recommend 15-25 words for clarity. Varying sentence length keeps readers engaged.

Does it count words too?

Yes, shows word count and average words per sentence.
