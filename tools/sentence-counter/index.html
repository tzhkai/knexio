Free Online Sentence Counter

This sentence counter helps you instantly calculate the number of sentences in your text. It also provides word count, character count, and estimated reading time.

Perfect for students, writers, bloggers, and SEO professionals who want clean and readable content.

How It Works

The tool detects sentence endings such as periods (.), question marks (?), and exclamation marks (!). It then splits the text and calculates results in real time.
