Frequently Asked Questions

Are the generated passwords truly random?

Yes, our generator uses cryptographically secure random numbers from your browser's Web Crypto API. This is the same level of randomness used for encryption and secure communications.

Is my password stored or transmitted?

No. All password generation happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server or stored anywhere. Your password never leaves your device.

What password length should I use?

We recommend at least 16 characters for maximum security. Longer passwords are exponentially harder to crack. You can generate passwords up to 64 characters long.

Should I use all character types?

Yes, using uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols creates the strongest passwords. However, if a service restricts certain characters, you can uncheck those options.

Can I use these passwords for my accounts?

Absolutely. These passwords are designed for maximum security. Use them for email, banking, social media, and any account where security matters.

How do I remember complex passwords?

We recommend using a password manager to securely store your generated passwords. Never write them down or share them with others. Most browsers and operating systems include built-in password managers.
