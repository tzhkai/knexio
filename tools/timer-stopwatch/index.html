Free Online Timer & Stopwatch - Versatile timekeeping tool that works in any browser. Set countdown timers for cooking, studying, workouts, or any activity. Use the stopwatch for tracking time with lap recording. Both modes feature clear displays and intuitive controls. No installation required - just open and use.

How It Works - The timer mode lets you set custom durations in hours, minutes, and seconds. Choose from preset quick buttons or enter your own time. When the countdown ends, you'll hear an audio alert. The stopwatch mode tracks elapsed time with millisecond precision. Record laps to track split times, perfect for sports or timing multiple events. All timing happens locally in your browser for accuracy and privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the timer work when I switch tabs?

Yes! Both the countdown timer and stopwatch continue running accurately even when you switch to another tab or minimize the browser. They use browser timing APIs that remain active in the background.

Will I hear the alarm if my volume is off?

No, the audio alert requires your device's volume to be on. Make sure your speakers or headphones are connected and volume is audible to hear the countdown completion sound.

How many laps can the stopwatch record?

The stopwatch can record unlimited laps. Each lap is displayed in a scrollable list, showing the lap number and time at the moment it was recorded. Use this for tracking multiple intervals or split times.

Can I use this for cooking or pomodoro?

Absolutely! The quick preset buttons (1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30 minutes) are perfect for common intervals like cooking timers or pomodoro technique. The 25-minute preset is ideal for pomodoro sessions.

Does this work on mobile devices?

Yes, the timer and stopwatch are fully responsive and work perfectly on smartphones and tablets. The touch-friendly buttons and large displays make it easy to use on any screen size.

Is my timing data stored or transmitted?

No. All timing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to any server or stored anywhere. Your timer settings and laps exist only while the page is open and are cleared when you close it.
