Free Online Sentence Counter

Count sentences in your text with real-time statistics. See total sentences, words, characters, and average sentence length for improved readability analysis.

How It Works

The tool detects sentence-ending punctuation marks (periods, exclamation points, and question marks) to count sentences. It excludes empty strings and whitespace-only text. Calculates average sentence length in words to assess readability, where 10-20 words per sentence is ideal for clear communication.
