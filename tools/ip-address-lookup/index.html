Frequently Asked Questions

What is an IP address?

An IP address (Internet Protocol address) is a unique numerical label assigned to each device connected to a computer network. It serves as your device's digital address, allowing websites and services to identify and communicate with you over the internet.

Is my IP address private information?

Your IP address reveals your approximate location and ISP but doesn't include personal details like your name or address. However, it can be used to track your online activities. Use VPNs if you want to mask your IP address for privacy.

Why does my location seem wrong?

IP geolocation is based on your ISP's registered location, which may differ from your actual physical location. This is especially common with mobile networks or if your ISP serves a large area. The location shown is approximate, not exact.

What's the difference between public and private IP?

Your public IP is assigned by your ISP and visible to the internet. Private IPs (like 192.168.x.x) are used within your local network and not visible externally. This tool shows your public IP address.

Can my IP address change?

Yes, most residential IP addresses are dynamic and can change when you restart your router or your ISP reassigns it. Business connections typically have static IPs that don't change. Click "Refresh" to see if your IP has changed.

Is this information stored or tracked?

No. All IP detection happens in real-time and no data is stored or logged. Your IP address is not recorded, and we don't track visitors. The information displayed is purely for your immediate use and disappears when you leave the page.
