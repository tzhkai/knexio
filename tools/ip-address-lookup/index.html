Tips for IP Lookup

Get more accurate results and understand what the data means:

  • Country-level data is reliable (~95%): City-level can be off by 50–100 miles. Use city results as a rough guide, not an exact location.
  • VPN and Proxy IPs Show Server Location: If someone is using a VPN, the lookup shows where the VPN server is — not where the person actually is.
  • IPv6 Works Too: This tool supports both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses. IPv6 lookups are increasingly important as adoption grows.
  • Check Your Own IP First: The page shows your IP automatically. Use it to verify the lookup is working before entering other addresses, and to confirm whether you're leaking your real IP.

What IP Lookup Can and Can't Tell You

IP geolocation is useful but has limits. Here's what to expect:

  • It can tell you: Country, region, city (approximate), ISP/organization, and timezone. Good for fraud detection, content localization, and network diagnostics.
  • It cannot tell you: Exact street address, the person's name, or their precise physical location. IP-based geolocation is not GPS — don't expect pin-point accuracy.
  • Dynamic IPs change: Most home users get a new IP address periodically. A lookup from yesterday may point to a different person today.
