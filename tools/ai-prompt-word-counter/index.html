About AI Prompt Counting

Paste or type your AI prompt above. Word count, character count, and token estimate are shown instantly.

Token count is estimated at approximately 1 token per 4 characters, which is how most AI models process English text.

How It Works

Different AI models have different token limits: ChatGPT-4o ~128K tokens, Claude 3 Opus 200K tokens, Gemini 1.5 Pro 1M tokens, Midjourney ~4,000 characters.

Use this tool to ensure your prompts fit within the token limits of your chosen AI model.
