How It Works

What's the difference from Word Counter?

Word Counter focuses on word-level analysis (density, top words, reading level). Text Analyzer provides comprehensive text-level statistics including sentences, paragraphs, syllables, and estimated reading time.

How is syllable count calculated?

Syllable count is estimated using a heuristic algorithm that counts vowel groups in English words. It is approximate but accurate for most common words.

Is my text sent to any server?

No. All text analysis happens entirely in your browser. Your content is never transmitted, stored, or logged.

What is estimated reading time?

Reading time is calculated at an average of 200–250 words per minute. The estimate adjusts based on the total word count of your text.
