Free Online Percentage Calculator

This percentage calculator helps you quickly solve common percentage problems. Calculate what percentage one number is of another, find a percentage of a value, or determine percentage increase or decrease.

Perfect for shopping discounts, tax calculations, grade calculations, and financial planning. All calculations happen instantly in your browser.

How It Works

The calculator provides three common percentage formulas. For "What is X% of Y?", it multiplies the percentage by the value. For "X is what % of Y?", it divides the part by the whole and multiplies by 100. For percentage change, it calculates the difference between two values relative to the original.

All calculations are performed instantly when you click the Calculate button. Results are displayed with two decimal places for accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example, to find 20% of 100, calculate (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?

Divide the first number (the part) by the second number (the whole), then multiply by 100. For example, 25 is 25% of 100 because (25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. For example, going from 100 to 150 is a 50% increase because ((150 - 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 50.

Is this percentage calculator free to use?

Yes, this percentage calculator is completely free. No registration or signup is required. Just open the page and start calculating.

Can I use this for calculating discounts?

Absolutely. To calculate a discounted price, use the "What is X% of Y?" calculator to find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price. For example, 20% off $100 means the discount is $20, so the final price is $80.

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server or stored anywhere.
