TikTok is one of the most used apps globally, but it can run into frustrating issues — from videos not loading to black screens and login failures. This guide covers the most common TikTok problems and their fixes for iPhone and Android, with detailed troubleshooting steps for each scenario.

Fix 1: TikTok Not Loading or Stuck on Loading Screen

If TikTok freezes on the loading screen or videos won't play, start with these steps:

Basic connection fixes

  1. Check if TikTok is down at isitdownrightnow.com or Downdetector.
  2. Toggle airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off — this resets your network connection.
  3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to identify if one network is the issue.
  4. Restart your router if on Wi-Fi (unplug for 30 seconds, then plug back in).
  5. Force-close TikTok and reopen it.
  6. Restart your phone completely.
Tip: If TikTok loads on mobile data but not Wi-Fi, the issue is your Wi-Fi network. Try forgetting and re-joining your Wi-Fi network, or check if your router has parental controls blocking TikTok.

Clear TikTok cache

  1. Open TikTok and tap Profile (bottom right).
  2. Tap the ≡ menu → Settings and Privacy.
  3. Scroll to Cache & Cellular Data → tap Clear Cache.
  4. Reopen TikTok.

Advanced network troubleshooting

  1. Check DNS settings: Some DNS servers block TikTok. Switch to Google DNS (8.8.8.8) or Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) in your Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Disable VPN: If you're using a VPN, try disconnecting it — some VPN IPs are blocked by TikTok.
  3. Check data saver mode: On Android, go to Settings > Network & Internet > Data Saver and ensure it's not restricting TikTok.
  4. Reset network settings: As a last resort, reset all network settings (this will erase saved Wi-Fi passwords).

Fix 2: TikTok Black Screen

A black screen while TikTok is open is usually a rendering or cache issue:

Steps to fix black screen

  1. Force-close TikTok completely (swipe it away from recent apps).
  2. Clear TikTok's cache (see Fix 1 above).
  3. Update TikTok from the App Store or Google Play.
  4. Restart your device.
  5. If the screen is black only in certain videos, the content may be loading slowly — wait a moment or check your connection speed.
  6. Check if your phone's battery saver or power saving mode is affecting app performance.
Tip: On Android, go to Settings → Apps → TikTok → Storage → Clear Cache (system-level cache, more thorough than in-app cache). On iPhone, you may need to offload and reinstall the app for a complete cache clear.

Black screen on specific content types

  1. Live streams black screen: This often indicates a region restriction or slow connection. Try switching networks.
  2. Effects causing black screen: Some AR effects require significant processing power. Close other apps to free up RAM.
  3. Green screen effects not working: Ensure TikTok has camera permissions and your device supports the required AR features.

Fix 3: No Sound on TikTok

Check mute settings

  1. Make sure your phone is not in silent/vibrate mode — check the physical mute switch (iPhone) or volume buttons.
  2. Turn up the media volume (not just ringer volume) with the volume buttons.
  3. Inside TikTok, tap the video — if a speaker icon with a line through it appears, tap it to unmute.
  4. Check that TikTok is not excluded from your sound profile: Android → Settings → Sound → App Volume.
  5. Disconnect Bluetooth headphones or speakers — audio may be routing there.

Fix audio permissions

  1. iPhone: Settings → TikTok → enable Microphone.
  2. Android: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Permissions → enable Microphone.
  3. If Bluetooth headphones are connected, the audio may be routing there — disconnect them or check their volume.
  4. Check if "Do Not Disturb" or "Focus" mode is active — these can suppress app audio.

Sound sync issues

  1. If sound is delayed or out of sync, close and reopen TikTok.
  2. Clear the app cache.
  3. Ensure your phone's operating system is up to date.
  4. Try recording in airplane mode with Wi-Fi on to reduce background processing.

Fix 4: TikTok Login Problems

Can't log in to TikTok

  1. Tap Forgot Password and reset via your email or phone number.
  2. Make sure your email or phone is verified on your TikTok account.
  3. If you use third-party login (Google, Apple, Facebook), make sure that account is working correctly.
  4. Check if your account has been temporarily restricted or suspended — TikTok usually sends an email notification.
  5. Try logging in from the TikTok website at tiktok.com to rule out app-specific issues.
  6. Ensure your device's date and time are set correctly — incorrect time settings can cause authentication failures.

Login error codes and their meanings

  1. "Too many attempts": Wait 15-30 minutes before trying again. TikTok temporarily locks login after multiple failed attempts.
  2. "Account not found": Double-check your username/email. You may have created the account with a different method (phone vs email).
  3. "Session expired": Simply log in again. This happens when your authentication token expires.
  4. "Network error": Check your internet connection and try again.
Warning: If you receive a message saying your account has been banned, you can appeal through the TikTok app: Profile → Settings → Report a Problem → Account Issue. Provide as much detail as possible in your appeal.

Fix 5: TikTok Keeps Crashing

Stop TikTok from crashing

  1. Update TikTok to the latest version — many crashes are caused by bugs fixed in updates.
  2. Free up phone storage. TikTok needs sufficient free space to operate smoothly. Delete unused apps or files. Aim for at least 1GB free space.
  3. Close background apps to free up RAM.
  4. Clear TikTok's cache and data (see Fix 1).
  5. Check if your phone's OS is up to date: Settings → Software Update.
  6. Disable battery optimization for TikTok (Android): Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → TikTok → Don't Optimize.

Crash on specific actions

  1. Crashes when opening camera: Check camera permissions and ensure no other app is using the camera.
  2. Crashes when applying effects: Your device may not support certain AR features. Try using simpler effects.
  3. Crashes when scrolling: This is often a memory issue. Close other apps and clear TikTok cache.
  4. Crashes on startup: Reinstall TikTok completely (see Fix 10).

Fix 6: Can't Upload Videos to TikTok

Video upload not working

  1. Confirm TikTok has storage permissions: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Permissions → Storage/Files.
  2. Check your internet speed — video uploads require a stable connection above 5 Mbps.
  3. Make sure the video is in a supported format (MP4, MOV) and under 4 GB.
  4. Try uploading on Wi-Fi instead of mobile data.
  5. If you get an "upload failed" error, reduce the video resolution or length and try again.
  6. Check if your account has any restrictions that prevent posting.

Video processing stuck

  1. If your video is stuck at "Processing", wait up to 10 minutes — longer videos take more time.
  2. Check your internet connection stability.
  3. Try uploading at a different time when server load may be lower.
  4. Split long videos into shorter segments.
  5. Ensure your video meets TikTok's content guidelines — violations can cause processing failures.

Fix 7: Comments Not Working

Comments not posting or loading

  1. Rate limiting: If you've posted many comments quickly, TikTok may temporarily limit you. Wait 15-30 minutes.
  2. Account restrictions: Check if your account has any strikes or restrictions that affect commenting.
  3. Comment section not loading: Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the video.
  4. Comments disabled: The video creator may have disabled comments on their post.
  5. Filtered comments: TikTok automatically filters comments that may violate guidelines. Check your filter settings in Privacy.

Fix 8: Following/Followers Issues

Can't follow users or followers not showing

  1. Following limit reached: TikTok has daily follow limits (typically around 200 per day). Wait 24 hours.
  2. Account restrictions: New accounts or accounts with violations may have limited following capabilities.
  3. Followers not updating: This is often a display delay. Close and reopen the app, or wait a few minutes.
  4. Can't unfollow: Similar rate limits apply to unfollowing. Space out your actions.
  5. Follow button not working: Clear cache or reinstall the app.

Fix 9: iPhone-Specific Fixes

iPhone Users: These steps are specifically optimized for iOS devices and iPhone-specific issues.

Complete iPhone troubleshooting

  1. Force-close TikTok: Swipe up from the bottom (iPhone X and newer) or double-click Home button (older iPhones), then swipe TikTok away.
  2. Check iOS version: Settings → General → Software Update. Install any available updates.
  3. Update TikTok: App Store → Profile icon → Scroll to pending updates → Update TikTok.
  4. Check Screen Time restrictions: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → make sure TikTok isn't blocked.
  5. Offload the app: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → TikTok → Offload App (keeps data but reinstalls the app).
  6. Reset all settings: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset All Settings (won't delete data).
  7. Check Background App Refresh: Settings → General → Background App Refresh → ensure TikTok is enabled.

Fix 10: Android-Specific Fixes

Android Users: These steps are specifically optimized for Android devices and manufacturer-specific issues.

Complete Android troubleshooting

  1. Force-stop TikTok: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Force Stop.
  2. Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Storage → Clear Cache.
  3. Clear app data (more thorough): Settings → Apps → TikTok → Storage → Clear Data (you'll need to log in again).
  4. Check Android version: Settings → About Phone → Software Update. Install any available updates.
  5. Update TikTok: Google Play Store → Profile → Manage apps & device → Updates available → Update TikTok.
  6. Disable battery optimization: Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → TikTok → Don't Optimize.
  7. Check app permissions: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Permissions → ensure Camera, Microphone, Storage, and Phone are allowed.
  8. Check Data Saver: Settings → Network & Internet → Data Saver → ensure TikTok is unrestricted.
  9. Manufacturer-specific issues: Some brands (Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung) have aggressive battery management. Add TikTok to "Unmonitored apps" or "Protected apps".

Fix 11: Clear Cache and Reinstall

If all else fails, a clean reinstall resolves most persistent TikTok issues:

Complete reinstall process

  1. Back up any drafts you want to keep (they may be lost during reinstall).
  2. Uninstall TikTok from your device.
  3. Android: Settings → Apps → TikTok → Uninstall. Then also go to Settings → Storage → Other Apps and clear any remaining TikTok data.
  4. iPhone: Press and hold the TikTok icon → Remove App → Delete App.
  5. Restart your device completely.
  6. Reinstall TikTok from the App Store or Google Play.
  7. Log back in and test.
Tip: After reinstalling, you may need to re-enable notifications and reconfigure your privacy settings. Your liked videos and following list are saved to your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common TikTok questions answered

Why is TikTok not loading videos?

TikTok videos may not load due to poor internet connection, TikTok server outages, full app cache, or an outdated app version. Try switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data, clear TikTok's cache in settings, and update the app.

Why does TikTok show a black screen?

A TikTok black screen is often caused by a cached data conflict, outdated app version, or a graphics rendering issue. Force-close the app, clear the cache (Settings > Privacy > Clear Cache inside TikTok), and restart your phone.

Why is there no sound on TikTok?

Check that your phone is not in silent mode or muted. Inside TikTok, make sure the video is not muted (tap the speaker icon). Also check Settings > Apps > TikTok > Permissions to ensure microphone and audio access are granted.

Why can't I log in to TikTok?

Login failures can be caused by an incorrect password, unverified email or phone, a temporarily suspended account, or TikTok server issues. Reset your password via 'Forgot Password' or wait a few minutes if TikTok servers are down.

Why does TikTok keep crashing?

TikTok crashes are usually caused by insufficient storage space, outdated app version, corrupted cache data, or software conflicts with other apps. Free up storage, update the app, and clear the cache to fix crashes.

Why can't I upload videos to TikTok?

Upload failures can be caused by poor internet connection, video format issues, storage permission problems, or file size limits. Ensure you have a stable connection, the video is under 4GB and in MP4/MOV format, and TikTok has storage permissions.

Why are my TikTok comments not posting?

Comment posting issues are often due to rate limiting (posting too many comments quickly), account restrictions, or network connectivity problems. Wait a few minutes and try again, or check if your account has any restrictions.

How do I fix TikTok on iPhone specifically?

On iPhone: force-close TikTok by swiping up and away, restart your iPhone, check for iOS updates in Settings > General > Software Update, update TikTok from the App Store, and if needed, offload the app in Settings > General > iPhone Storage > TikTok > Offload App.

How do I fix TikTok on Android specifically?

On Android: force-stop TikTok in Settings > Apps > TikTok > Force Stop, clear cache in Settings > Apps > TikTok > Storage > Clear Cache, restart your phone, update TikTok from Google Play Store, and ensure the app has all necessary permissions.

Why is TikTok banned in my country and how can I access it?

TikTok is banned in some countries due to regulatory decisions. To access TikTok in these regions, you may need to use a VPN service to connect to a server in a country where TikTok is available. However, be aware that circumventing bans may violate local laws.
