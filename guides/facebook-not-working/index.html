Facebook is one of the most widely used apps in the world — so when it stops working, it's frustrating. Whether the app is crashing, the news feed won't refresh, or you can't log in, this guide covers every common fix with detailed troubleshooting steps for all devices.

1. Facebook Not Loading

Before trying any fixes, check whether Facebook is experiencing a server outage. Visit Downdetector or search "Facebook down" on Twitter/X to see if others are affected.

Quick Fix Checklist

  1. Check your internet connection — open another website or app to confirm it's working.
  2. Restart the Facebook app completely (force-close it first).
  3. Restart your phone or computer.
  4. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  5. Check if Facebook is down at Downdetector.
  6. Check your DNS settings — try switching to Google DNS (8.8.8.8) or Cloudflare (1.1.1.1).

Advanced loading fixes

  1. Disable VPN: If you're using a VPN, try disconnecting it temporarily.
  2. Check date and time: Incorrect system time can cause SSL certificate errors. Set to automatic.
  3. Flush DNS cache: On Windows, open Command Prompt as admin and run ipconfig /flushdns.
  4. Check hosts file: On PC, ensure facebook.com isn't blocked in your hosts file.
  5. Disable proxy settings: Check if a proxy is configured that's blocking Facebook.
Tip: If Facebook loads on one device but not another, the issue is device-specific, not a server outage. Focus on the problematic device's settings.

2. News Feed Not Refreshing

A stuck or blank Facebook news feed is usually caused by a corrupted app cache or a network issue. Try these fixes in order:

Feed refresh fixes

  1. Pull to refresh – Scroll to the top of the feed and pull down to force a refresh.
  2. Force-close and reopen the Facebook app.
  3. Clear app cache – On Android: Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage → Clear Cache. On iPhone, clear cache via Settings → Facebook → or reinstall the app.
  4. Switch network – Toggle between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  5. Update the app – Open the App Store or Google Play and install any pending updates for Facebook.
  6. Check feed preferences: Tap the menu → Settings → News Feed Preferences to ensure you haven't accidentally hidden content.

Feed display issues

  1. "Most Recent" vs "Top Stories": Tap the menu (≡) and switch between these views to see if content appears.
  2. Check filters: Ensure you haven't applied any content filters that hide posts.
  3. Unfollow/Follow: If specific people's posts are missing, check if you're still following them.
  4. Group feeds: For group content not showing, visit the group directly to verify posts exist.

3. Facebook Login Problems

If you can't log in to Facebook, there are several possible causes:

Forgot Password

  1. Click Forgot Password on the login screen.
  2. Enter your email, phone number, or username associated with the account.
  3. Facebook will send a reset link to your email or phone number.
  4. Follow the link and create a new password.
  5. Log in with your new password.

Two-Factor Authentication Issues

  1. If you lost access to your 2FA device, use a recovery code (saved during setup).
  2. Go to the Facebook Help Center to regain account access.
  3. You may need to verify your identity with ID.
  4. Consider setting up backup 2FA methods for future issues.

Account Disabled or Locked

  1. If Facebook disabled your account for violating Community Standards, you'll see a specific message at login.
  2. You can submit an appeal through the Facebook Appeals page.
  3. Provide any requested documentation to verify your identity.
  4. Wait for Facebook's review — this can take several days.
Warning: If you're being asked to verify your identity via video selfie, this is a legitimate Facebook security check, not a phishing attempt. However, always ensure you're on facebook.com before entering credentials.

4. Facebook Messenger Not Working

Messenger-specific fixes

  1. Update Messenger: Check App Store or Google Play for updates.
  2. Clear cache: Settings → Apps → Messenger → Storage → Clear Cache (Android).
  3. Check notifications: Ensure Messenger notifications are enabled in your phone settings.
  4. Try Messenger Lite: Download the lightweight version if the main app keeps crashing.
  5. Check message requests: Messages from non-friends go to Message Requests.
  6. Secret Conversations: If you can't see messages, check if they were sent as Secret Conversations on another device.

5. Can't Post or Share on Facebook

Posting issues

  1. Check account restrictions: You may be temporarily blocked from posting. Check your Support Inbox.
  2. Rate limiting: Posting too quickly can trigger temporary limits. Wait a few hours.
  3. Content violations: Ensure your post follows Community Standards.
  4. Photo/video issues: Check file size limits (photos under 8MB, videos under 10GB).
  5. Privacy settings: Verify your post's privacy setting allows the intended audience to see it.
  6. Try different content: Test with a simple text post to isolate the issue.

6. Facebook Not Working on iPhone or Android

Mobile Device Fixes: These steps address app-specific issues on smartphones.

iPhone Fixes

  1. Force-close Facebook: swipe up from bottom and swipe away the Facebook card.
  2. Check for iOS updates: Settings → General → Software Update.
  3. Update Facebook from the App Store.
  4. Go to Settings → Facebook → toggle Background App Refresh on.
  5. Check Screen Time restrictions: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  6. Delete and reinstall the Facebook app.
  7. Reset network settings if persistent: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset → Reset Network Settings.

Android Fixes

  1. Force-stop Facebook: Settings → Apps → Facebook → Force Stop.
  2. Clear cache and data: Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage → Clear Cache.
  3. Check for Android system updates and Facebook app updates.
  4. Uninstall and reinstall Facebook from Google Play.
  5. Try the Facebook Lite app if the main app continues crashing.
  6. Disable battery optimization: Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → Facebook → Don't Optimize.
  7. Check Data Saver settings: Settings → Network → Data Saver → ensure Facebook is unrestricted.

7. Facebook Not Working in Browser

If Facebook fails in your web browser, try these steps:

Browser troubleshooting

  1. Clear browser cache – In Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → All time → Clear data. In Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.
  2. Disable extensions – Open Chrome in Incognito (Ctrl+Shift+N) and try Facebook. If it works, a browser extension is the culprit.
  3. Try a different browser – Switch from Chrome to Firefox or Edge to isolate the issue.
  4. Change DNS – Set your DNS to 8.8.8.8 (Google) or 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare).
  5. Update browser – Ensure you're using the latest version of your browser.
  6. Disable hardware acceleration – Chrome Settings → Advanced → System → uncheck "Use hardware acceleration".

8. Facebook Videos Not Playing

Facebook videos buffering or showing a black screen are common complaints. The most effective fixes:

Video playback fixes

  1. Disable hardware acceleration in Chrome (Settings → Advanced → System → uncheck "Use hardware acceleration").
  2. Update your graphics driver on Windows (Device Manager → Display Adapters → Update driver).
  3. Lower video quality: click the ⚙ gear icon on any video → Video Quality → choose a lower resolution.
  4. Try a different browser.
  5. Check internet speed — videos need at least 3 Mbps for SD, 5 Mbps for HD.
  6. Clear browser cache and cookies.
  7. Disable browser extensions that might block video content.

9. Facebook Running Slow

Speed up Facebook

  1. Close other apps/tabs: Free up RAM by closing unnecessary programs.
  2. Clear cache: Regularly clear Facebook app or browser cache.
  3. Use Facebook Lite: The lightweight app is faster on slower connections.
  4. Check internet speed: Run a speed test to ensure adequate bandwidth.
  5. Disable autoplay videos: Facebook Settings → Media → Autoplay → Never.
  6. Reduce image quality: Settings → Media → turn on "Upload HD" only when needed.
  7. Update the app: Newer versions often have performance improvements.

10. Account Issues

Hacked or compromised account

  1. Go to facebook.com/hacked immediately.
  2. Follow the steps to secure your account.
  3. Change your password and log out of all devices.
  4. Enable two-factor authentication.
  5. Check recent login activity and remove unrecognized devices.
  6. Review apps with access to your Facebook account and remove suspicious ones.

Reporting problems to Facebook

  1. In the app: Menu → Help & Support → Report a Problem.
  2. On web: Click the ? icon → Report a Problem.
  3. Be specific about the issue and include screenshots if possible.
  4. For business accounts, use Facebook Business Help Center for priority support.

11. Reinstall Facebook

If none of the above fixes work, a clean reinstall usually resolves persistent bugs or corrupt data:

Complete reinstall process

  1. Back up any important information (photos, messages) if needed.
  2. Uninstall Facebook from your device.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Clear any remaining data (Android: Settings → Storage → Clear residual files).
  5. Reinstall Facebook from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  6. Log in and test.
Tip: After reinstalling, do not restore from backup — start fresh and only re-enable notifications and other settings manually to avoid reintroducing corrupted data.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Common Facebook questions answered

Why is Facebook not loading?

Facebook may not load due to server outages, slow internet, outdated app, corrupted cache, or DNS issues. Check downdetector.com to see if Facebook is down for everyone.

Why is my Facebook news feed not refreshing?

A stuck news feed is usually caused by a poor internet connection or a corrupted app cache. Try force-closing the app and reopening it, or clear the Facebook app cache in your device settings.

Why can't I log into Facebook?

Login failures on Facebook are commonly caused by incorrect password, two-factor authentication issues, or account restrictions. Use the 'Forgot Password' link to reset your password or visit the Facebook Help Center.

How do I fix Facebook on iPhone?

On iPhone: force-close the Facebook app, check your internet connection, update Facebook from the App Store, and restart your iPhone. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the Facebook app.

Why is Facebook Messenger not working?

Messenger issues are often caused by the same factors affecting Facebook — poor connection, outdated app, or server problems. Try updating Messenger, clearing its cache, or using Messenger Lite as an alternative.

Why can't I post on Facebook?

Posting issues can be caused by account restrictions, rate limiting, content violations, or technical glitches. Check if you've been temporarily blocked from posting, ensure your content follows community standards, and try again later.

How do I fix Facebook videos not playing?

Video playback issues can be fixed by updating the app, clearing cache, checking internet speed, disabling browser extensions, or updating graphics drivers on PC. Try lowering video quality if bandwidth is limited.

Why is Facebook so slow?

Facebook may run slowly due to poor internet connection, too many browser tabs open, outdated app, or device performance issues. Close unnecessary apps/tabs, clear cache, and ensure you have a stable internet connection.

How do I contact Facebook support?

Facebook offers limited direct support. Try the Help Center at facebook.com/help, use the Report a Problem feature in the app, or visit the Facebook Community forums. For business accounts, Facebook Business Support may be available.

Why was my Facebook account disabled?

Accounts are disabled for violating Facebook's Community Standards, suspicious activity, or using a fake name. You can appeal the decision through the notification you received or at facebook.com/help/contact/260749603972907
