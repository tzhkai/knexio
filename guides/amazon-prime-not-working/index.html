Amazon Prime Not Working? Here's How to Fix It

Amazon Prime not loading, buffering, or showing playback errors? This guide covers fixes for TV, Firestick, mobile apps, and web browsers.
Quick check: Visit Amazon Web Services Status. If AWS or Amazon Prime is down, wait it out. Also check Downdetector.

Why Amazon Prime Video Fails

  • Unstable or slow internet connection
  • Outdated Amazon Prime app version
  • Temporary Amazon Prime server outage
  • Device date/time settings incorrect
  • VPN blocking Prime content
  • Expired or inactive Prime subscription
  • Cached app data causing playback errors
  • Device storage full
  • Geographic content restrictions

Error Codes Explained

Error Code Meaning Fix
4592 Device limitation or playback restriction Check device authorization, try a different device
7003 Unable to play video Restart app, clear cache, check internet connection
7202 Network timeout or proxy detected Disable VPN, restart router, check firewall
7203 Network connection lost Check Wi-Fi/Ethernet connection, restart router
7305 Playback device issue Reauthorize device, check HDCP compatibility
8075 App data corruption Clear app data and cache, reinstall if needed
8147 DRM or licensing error Check account subscription, sign out and back in
9068 Playback error Clear browser cache, disable extensions, try different browser
9085 Web player error Clear cookies, enable JavaScript, try incognito mode

Step-by-Step Fixes

Step 1 — Check Internet Connection

Restart your router and modem. Run a speed test – you need at least 5 Mbps for HD streaming. For 4K, aim for 25 Mbps. Test on fast.com or your ISP's speed test site.

Step 2 — Restart Amazon Prime App or Browser

On TV/Firestick: Go to Settings → Restart App. On mobile: Close app fully and reopen. On browser: Refresh page or try incognito mode.

Step 3 — Clear Prime App Cache (Mobile & TV)

On Android TV: Settings → Apps → Amazon Prime → Clear Cache & Data. On Firestick: Settings → Applications → Prime Video → Clear Cache. On iOS: Uninstall and reinstall app.

Step 4 — Verify Prime Subscription

Visit Amazon Prime Central. Confirm your subscription is active and your payment method is valid. Check for failed payment notifications.

Step 5 — Disable VPN

If you're using a VPN, disable it. Amazon Prime blocks most VPNs due to content licensing. Some premium VPNs have "streaming-optimized" servers that may work.

Step 6 — Lower Video Quality

On Prime Video: Settings → Playback → Video Quality. Set to "Good" or "Better" to reduce buffering. Switch to "Best" only on stable connections.

Step 7 — Update Amazon Prime App

On your device's app store, check for Amazon Prime updates. Outdated apps often have playback bugs. Uninstall and reinstall if update doesn't fix it.

Step 8 — Sign Out and Back In

On Prime: Settings → Sign Out. Wait 30 seconds, then sign back in. This refreshes your account tokens and often fixes "something went wrong" errors.

Device-Specific Fixes

Smart TV (Samsung, LG, Sony, Vizio, TCL, Hisense)

  • Check your TV's date and time settings – set to automatic
  • Delete and reinstall the Amazon Prime app
  • Update your TV firmware (Settings → Support → Software Update varies by brand)
  • Try Ethernet instead of Wi-Fi for more stable connection
  • Check HDCP compatibility – older TVs may not support newer DRM
  • Factory reset the TV as a last resort (note your settings first)

Firestick / Fire TV

  • Hold Select + Play for 10 seconds to restart Firestick
  • Clear cache in Settings → Applications → Prime Video
  • Disable VPN apps if installed on the device
  • Use Amazon Ethernet Adapter for better connection
  • Factory reset: Settings → My Fire TV → Reset to Factory Defaults
  • Update Fire OS: Settings → My Fire TV → About → Check for Updates

Apple TV

  • Check for tvOS updates: Settings → System → Software Updates
  • Delete and reinstall Prime Video from the App Store
  • Force quit: double-click TV button, swipe up on Prime Video
  • Restart Apple TV: Settings → System → Restart
  • Ensure your Apple ID country is set correctly for Prime content

Roku

  • Remove and re-add the Prime Video channel
  • Restart the channel: Home → Prime Video → * → Restart
  • Check system update: Settings → System → System Update
  • Factory reset channel: Home → Prime Video → * → Reset
  • Restart Roku: Settings → System → Power → System Restart

Mobile (iOS & Android)

  • Force-close Prime app and reopen
  • Clear app cache in device settings
  • Update Prime app from App Store/Play Store
  • Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data
  • Check that cellular data is enabled for Prime Video
  • On Android: Settings → Apps → Prime Video → Data usage → check Unrestricted

Web Browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

  • Clear browser cache and cookies for Amazon sites
  • Try incognito/private mode to rule out extension conflicts
  • Disable VPN or proxy extensions
  • Make sure JavaScript and cookies are enabled
  • Update your browser to the latest version
  • Try a different browser to isolate the issue

Network and Bandwidth Troubleshooting

Amazon Prime Video requires a stable, fast internet connection. Network issues are the most common cause of streaming problems.

Speed Requirements

  • SD quality: 1 Mbps
  • HD quality: 5 Mbps (Amazon recommends 15+ Mbps)
  • 4K Ultra HD: 25 Mbps

Optimize Your Connection

  1. Use a wired Ethernet connection instead of Wi-Fi when possible
  2. Move your streaming device closer to your Wi-Fi router
  3. Switch from 2.4 GHz to 5 GHz Wi-Fi band for faster speeds
  4. Restart your router and modem
  5. Reduce network congestion by disconnecting other devices
  6. Check for firmware updates on your router

Change DNS Settings

ISP DNS can sometimes cause streaming issues. Try using Google DNS or Cloudflare:

  1. Windows: Control Panel → Network → Change Adapter Settings → IPv4 → DNS: 8.8.8.8 / 8.8.4.4
  2. Mac: System Settings → Network → DNS → add 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8
  3. Router: Access router settings → DNS settings → update primary DNS

Firewall and Proxy Settings

  1. Corporate or school networks may block Prime Video
  2. Check firewall rules that may be preventing streaming
  3. VPNs often don't work with Prime Video – try disabling
  4. Some proxy services are blocked by Amazon

Account Status Checks

Some Prime Video issues stem from your Amazon account or subscription status.

Verify Your Prime Subscription

  1. Go to amazon.com/prime and sign in
  2. Check "Your Prime Membership" page for status and renewal date
  3. Verify your payment method is valid and up to date
  4. Look for failed payment notifications in your email
  5. If you have Prime Student, verify your enrollment is still active

Manage Device Authorization

  1. Go to amazon.com/gp/managec | /yourcontent/library to view registered devices
  2. Prime Video allows streaming on multiple devices simultaneously
  3. If you've hit the device limit, deauthorize old or unused devices
  4. Family sharing: Prime benefits can be shared with household members on Amazon Household

Check for Payment Issues

  1. Go to amazon.com/gp/history → Payment Transaction History
  2. Update expired credit cards or invalid payment methods
  3. If payment fails, Prime features may be temporarily restricted
  4. Check that your billing address matches your payment method

Regional Content Issues

Amazon Prime Video content varies by country/region due to licensing agreements and local regulations. Your account's region is typically determined by your payment method's country and default delivery address.

Why Content Differs by Region

  • Amazon licenses content separately for each country
  • Some shows/movies are only available in certain regions
  • Local regulations affect content availability
  • Sports streaming rights vary significantly by region
  • Amazon Originals may have different availability worldwide

Check Your Account Region

  1. Go to amazon.com/gp/customer-preferences/ to see your marketplace
  2. Your payment method country determines your primary region
  3. Prime membership is country-specific – UK Prime ≠ US Prime
  4. Delivery address country also affects content library

Accessing Prime Video While Traveling

  1. Amazon should automatically show your home library when traveling abroad temporarily
  2. Some content may still be restricted based on where you're physically located
  3. Downloaded titles for offline viewing may not be available in all regions
  4. If you move permanently, you may need to create a new account for the new country

VPN and Prime Video

  1. Amazon actively blocks most VPN services
  2. Some premium VPNs with dedicated streaming servers may work
  3. Using VPNs to bypass regional restrictions may violate Amazon's terms
  4. If VPN is detected, you may see error 7202 or content may be unavailable
Warning: Using VPNs or other methods to bypass regional restrictions may violate Amazon's Terms of Service. Amazon may restrict access to your account if violations are detected. Use at your own discretion.

Content Not Available in Your Region

  1. If you see "This title is not available in your location," it's a regional restriction
  2. Some titles may be available on different Amazon marketplaces (e.g., .co.uk vs .com)
  3. Check if an alternate version (dubbed/subtitled) is available
  4. Content may become available later as licensing agreements change

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Amazon Prime keep buffering?

Mostly due to slow internet. Restart your router, move closer to router, or use Ethernet. Lower video quality also helps. Amazon recommends at least 5 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K.

Is Amazon Prime down right now?

Check AWS Status Page or Downdetector.

Can I watch Prime without internet?

No, Amazon Prime requires an active internet connection for streaming. Some titles can be downloaded for offline viewing on mobile devices via the Prime Video app.

Why does Prime say something went wrong?

This is a generic error. Try: clearing cache, signing out/in, updating app, or checking your subscription status. The error may also be caused by VPN or proxy detection.

How many devices can stream Prime Video?

Amazon Prime Video allows streaming on up to 3 devices simultaneously with the same account. However, the number of devices you can register is unlimited.
