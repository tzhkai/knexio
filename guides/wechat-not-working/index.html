WeChat Not Working? 12 Ways to Fix It (2026)

📅 Updated March 2026 ⏱️ 15 min read 🛠️ 12 Solutions

Is WeChat not working, failing to log in, or not sending messages? You're not alone. WeChat is one of the most popular messaging apps globally, but like any app, it can experience issues. This comprehensive guide will walk you through 12 proven solutions to get WeChat working again on your iPhone or Android device.

⚠️ Important: Before trying these fixes, make sure you remember your WeChat password and have access to your registered phone number. Some solutions may require you to log in again.

Common WeChat Problems

  • WeChat won't open or keeps crashing
  • "Login failed" or "Unable to login" errors
  • Messages not sending or receiving
  • "Connection failed" or network errors
  • WeChat stuck on loading screen
  • Voice/video calls not working
  • Moments not loading

How to Fix WeChat Not Working

1

Check WeChat Server Status

Before troubleshooting your device, verify if WeChat's servers are down:

  • Check Downdetector for outage reports
  • Search "WeChat down" on Twitter/X for real-time user reports
  • Ask friends if they're experiencing similar issues

If WeChat's servers are down, you'll need to wait for them to resolve the issue.

2

Check Your Internet Connection

WeChat requires a stable internet connection to function:

  1. Turn off Wi-Fi and try using mobile data (or vice versa)
  2. Run a speed test to verify your connection
  3. Restart your router if on Wi-Fi
  4. Check if other apps can access the internet
  5. Disable VPN if you're using one (WeChat may block some VPNs)
3

Force Close and Restart WeChat

For iPhone:

  1. Swipe up from the bottom (or double-click Home button on older iPhones)
  2. Find WeChat in the app switcher
  3. Swipe up on WeChat to close it
  4. Reopen WeChat from your home screen

For Android:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → WeChat
  2. Tap "Force Stop"
  3. Reopen WeChat

4

Restart Your Device

A simple restart can resolve many app issues:

  1. Power off your device completely
  2. Wait 10-15 seconds
  3. Power it back on
  4. Try opening WeChat again
5

Update WeChat to the Latest Version

Outdated apps can cause compatibility issues:

  1. Open App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android)
  2. Search for "WeChat"
  3. If an update is available, tap "Update"
  4. Wait for the update to complete
  5. Restart WeChat
6

Clear WeChat Cache

Corrupted cache files can cause app crashes:

For iPhone:

  1. Open WeChat → Me → Settings → General
  2. Tap "Storage"
  3. Tap "Clear WeChat Cache"
  4. Confirm the action

For Android:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → WeChat
  2. Tap "Storage"
  3. Tap "Clear Cache" (don't clear data)
  4. Restart WeChat
7

Check Date and Time Settings

Incorrect date/time can prevent WeChat from connecting:

  1. Open Settings on your device
  2. Go to General → Date & Time (iPhone) or System → Date & Time (Android)
  3. Enable "Set Automatically"
  4. Restart WeChat

8

Re-login to Your WeChat Account

Sometimes logging out and back in resolves authentication issues:

  1. Open WeChat → Me → Settings
  2. Scroll down and tap "Log Out"
  3. Confirm logout
  4. Re-enter your phone number and password
  5. Complete any verification steps
💡 Tip: Make sure you know your password before logging out. If you've forgotten it, use the "Forgot Password" option.
9

Check for iOS/Android System Updates

Your device's operating system may need updating:

  1. Open Settings → General → Software Update (iPhone)
  2. Or Settings → System → System Update (Android)
  3. If an update is available, download and install it
  4. Restart your device after updating
10

Reinstall WeChat

If other solutions don't work, try reinstalling:

⚠️ Warning: Uninstalling WeChat will delete local chat history unless you've backed it up. Make sure to back up your chats first!

Backup chats before uninstalling:

  1. WeChat → Me → Settings → Chat → Chat Backup
  2. Tap "Backup" to save to WeChat's servers

To reinstall:

  1. Uninstall WeChat from your device
  2. Restart your device
  3. Download WeChat from App Store or Google Play
  4. Install and log in
  5. Restore chat history if needed
11

Check Account Status

Your account may be restricted or blocked:

  • If you see "This WeChat account has been blocked," you'll need to appeal the block
  • Visit WeChat's account recovery page
  • Follow the verification process
  • Contact WeChat support if needed
Error: "Your account has been blocked due to suspicious activity"
12

Contact WeChat Support

If none of the above solutions work:

  • Open WeChat → Me → Settings → Help & Feedback
  • Or visit WeChat Help Center
  • Submit a support ticket with details about your issue
  • Include screenshots of any error messages

Prevention Tips

  • Keep WeChat updated: Enable automatic updates
  • Regular backups: Back up chats monthly
  • Don't use unofficial mods: They can cause issues and security risks
  • Monitor storage: Keep at least 1GB free space on your device
  • Use official app stores: Only download from App Store or Google Play

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is WeChat not working today?

WeChat may not be working due to server outages, internet connection issues, app bugs, or account problems. Check WeChat's server status first before troubleshooting your device.

How do I fix WeChat login failed error?

Try these solutions:

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Ensure your phone number is correctly entered
  3. Reset your WeChat password if needed
  4. Clear WeChat cache
  5. Reinstall the app

Why won't WeChat send messages?

Messages won't send if:

  • You have no internet connection
  • WeChat servers are down
  • The recipient has blocked you
  • Your account is restricted
  • You're using a VPN that's blocked by WeChat

How do I recover deleted WeChat chats?

Deleted chats can be recovered if you've backed them up:

  1. Go to WeChat → Me → Settings → Chat → Chat Backup
  2. Tap "Restore" to recover from backup
  3. If no backup exists, chats cannot be recovered

Why does WeChat keep crashing?

WeChat crashes can be caused by:

  • Corrupted cache files
  • Outdated app version
  • Insufficient storage space
  • System incompatibility
  • Conflicting apps

Try clearing cache, updating WeChat, or reinstalling the app.

Can I use WeChat on multiple devices?

WeChat is primarily designed for mobile use. Desktop versions exist but require mobile app login. You can be logged in on one mobile device and one desktop simultaneously, but not on multiple mobile devices.

How do I check if WeChat is down?

Check:

  • Downdetector.com for outage reports
  • Twitter/X for #WeChatDown trending
  • WeChat's official social media accounts
  • Ask friends if they're having issues

What should I do if my WeChat account is blocked?

If your account is blocked:

  1. Follow the appeal process in WeChat
  2. Submit verification documents as requested
  3. Contact WeChat support through Help & Feedback
  4. Wait for the review process (usually 1-3 days)

Why can't I receive WeChat verification code?

If you're not receiving SMS codes:

  • Check your phone's signal strength
  • Verify you're not in a blocked region
  • Check if your carrier blocks international SMS
  • Wait 60 seconds before requesting another code
  • Contact your carrier if the issue persists

How much data does WeChat use?

WeChat data usage varies:

  • Text messages: ~1KB per message
  • Voice messages: ~100KB per minute
  • Video calls: ~5-15MB per minute
  • Moments photos: ~500KB-2MB per photo
