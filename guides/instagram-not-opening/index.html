Instagram Not Opening? Here's How to Fix It

Instagram app won't open, keeps crashing, or fails to launch on iPhone or Android? This guide covers proven step-by-step fixes for every scenario — from app crashes on startup to a blank screen that freezes when you tap the icon.
Quick test: Open Instagram.com in your browser. If it loads fine, the problem is app-specific. If the browser also fails, it's likely an Instagram server outage or internet issue.

Why Instagram Won't Open

  • Corrupted Instagram app files from incomplete updates
  • Outdated Instagram app version with known bugs
  • iOS or Android system software bug
  • Low device storage space (less than 1GB free)
  • Instagram server outage or maintenance
  • Corrupted app cache or data
  • Conflicting apps or background processes
  • VPN or proxy interfering with the connection
  • Date and time set incorrectly on your device
  • Restricted profile or account issue
  • Using an unauthorized or modified Instagram app
  • Outdated operating system on your device

Step-by-Step Fixes

Step 1 — Force-Close Instagram Completely

Make sure Instagram is fully closed, not just minimized:

  • On iPhone with Face ID: Swipe up from the bottom edge and pause in the middle of the screen → find Instagram preview → swipe up on it to close
  • On iPhone with Home button: Double-click the Home button → find Instagram → swipe up to close
  • On Android: Swipe up from the bottom and hold to open recent apps → find Instagram → swipe it away. Alternatively: go to Settings → Apps → Instagram → Force Stop

Wait at least 10 seconds before trying to reopen Instagram. This gives your device time to fully clear the app from memory.

Step 2 — Restart Your Device

A full device restart resolves most temporary glitches that prevent apps from opening:

  • On iPhone (Face ID): Press and hold the side button + either volume button → drag the power slider → wait 20 seconds → press and hold the side button until you see the Apple logo
  • On iPhone (Home button): Press and hold the side (or top) button → drag the slider → wait 20 seconds → press and hold the button until the Apple logo appears
  • On Android: Press and hold the power button → tap Restart (or hold power for 30 seconds to force restart if the screen is frozen)

Step 3 — Clear Instagram Cache (Android)

Corrupted cache data is one of the most common reasons Instagram won't open on Android:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Apps (or "Apps & notifications")
  3. Search for and tap Instagram
  4. Tap Storage & cache (or just "Storage")
  5. Tap Clear Cache
  6. Try opening Instagram again

If clearing cache doesn't help, try Clear Storage (or "Clear Data"). This is more thorough — it will log you out and reset the app, but won't delete your account.

Step 4 — Offload Instagram (iPhone Only)

iOS doesn't let you clear cache directly, but offloading the app achieves the same result:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Go to General → iPhone Storage
  3. Scroll down and tap Instagram
  4. Tap Offload App
  5. Confirm by tapping Offload App again
  6. After offloading completes, tap Reinstall App

Offloading removes the app's files and cache while keeping your account data, photos, and messages intact. When you reinstall, you'll be logged in automatically.

Step 5 — Check for iOS / Android System Updates

An outdated operating system can cause compatibility issues with Instagram:

  • On iPhone: Go to Settings → General → Software Update → if an update is available, tap Download and Install. Make sure your iPhone is plugged in and connected to Wi-Fi.
  • On Android: Go to Settings → System → Software Update (or "System updates") → tap Check for updates → install any available updates. The exact path varies by manufacturer (Samsung, Pixel, etc.).

Step 6 — Update Instagram App

An outdated app version may have bugs that prevent it from opening:

  • On iPhone: Open the App Store → tap your profile picture (top right) → scroll down → if Instagram has an "Update" button, tap it. Or search for "Instagram" and tap "Update."
  • On Android: Open the Play Store → tap your profile picture → "Manage apps & device" → "Updates available" → find Instagram and tap "Update."

Step 7 — Free Up Device Storage

Instagram needs significant free storage to function. If your device is nearly full, the app may fail to open:

  • On iPhone: Go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → review which apps are using the most space. Delete apps you don't use, remove large photo/video files, or offload unused apps.
  • On Android: Go to Settings → Storage → review storage usage. Tap "Free up space" to remove cached files, duplicate photos, and unused apps. Ensure you have at least 2–3GB of free space.

Instagram itself can consume 1–2GB of storage over time. If it's taking up too much space, offloading (iOS) or clearing data (Android) will reclaim it.

Step 8 — Toggle Airplane Mode

Resetting your network connection can fix launch issues caused by network-related hangs:

  1. Swipe down from the top-right (iPhone) or top (Android) to open Control Center
  2. Tap the Airplane Mode icon to enable it
  3. Wait at least 30 seconds
  4. Tap Airplane Mode again to disable it
  5. Wait 10 seconds for your network to reconnect
  6. Try opening Instagram again

Step 9 — Disable VPN or Proxy

Some VPNs block or interfere with Instagram's servers, preventing the app from launching:

  1. Open your device Settings
  2. Go to VPN (iPhone) or Network & Internet → VPN (Android)
  3. Disconnect or toggle off your VPN connection
  4. Also check your VPN app itself and disconnect from there
  5. Try opening Instagram

Step 10 — Check Date and Time Settings

Incorrect date or time settings can prevent Instagram from establishing a secure connection:

  • On iPhone: Go to Settings → General → Date & Time → enable Set Automatically
  • On Android: Go to Settings → System → Date & time → enable Automatic date & time and Automatic time zone

Step 11 — Delete and Reinstall Instagram

This is the most thorough fix and resolves nearly all app-related issues:

  • On iPhone: Press and hold the Instagram icon → tap Remove App → tap Delete App → confirm with Delete. Then open the App Store, search "Instagram," and tap the download icon.
  • On Android: Press and hold the Instagram icon → tap Uninstall → confirm. Then open the Play Store, search "Instagram," and tap Install.

Will I lose my account? No — your account, photos, followers, and messages are stored on Instagram's servers, not on your device. After reinstalling, simply log in with your username and password. Your profile will be exactly as you left it.

Step 12 — Check Instagram Server Status

If nothing works, Instagram itself may be experiencing an outage:

  • Visit Instagram.com in your browser — if it doesn't load there either, it's a server issue
  • Check Downdetector for Instagram for real-time outage reports
  • Search "Instagram down" on Twitter/X to see if others are reporting the same issue
  • Visit IsItDownRightNow for server status

If Instagram is down, the only option is to wait. Most outages are resolved within a few hours.

Device-Specific Fixes

📱 iPhone / iPad — Complete Troubleshooting Path

  1. Force-close Instagram: Swipe up from bottom → find Instagram → swipe up to close
  2. Restart iPhone: Hold side + volume → drag slider → wait 20s → hold side button until Apple logo
  3. Offload app: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Instagram → Offload App → Reinstall App
  4. Update app: App Store → Profile → Update next to Instagram
  5. Update iOS: Settings → General → Software Update → Download and Install
  6. Check storage: Settings → General → iPhone Storage — ensure at least 2GB free
  7. Fix date/time: Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically ON
  8. Disable VPN: Settings → VPN → toggle OFF
  9. Toggle Airplane Mode: Swipe down → Airplane Mode ON for 30s → OFF
  10. Delete and reinstall: Long-press Instagram → Remove App → Delete App → reinstall from App Store

Additional iPhone tips:

  • If you're on an older iPhone model (iPhone 8 or earlier), make sure iOS is updated to the latest supported version
  • Check if the issue started after an iOS update — if so, Instagram may need time to release a compatible update
  • If Instagram crashes immediately on launch, try offloading first before a full reinstall

🤖 Android — Complete Troubleshooting Path

  1. Force-stop Instagram: Settings → Apps → Instagram → Force Stop
  2. Restart Android: Hold power → Restart (or hold power 30s for force restart)
  3. Clear cache: Settings → Apps → Instagram → Storage → Clear Cache
  4. Clear data (if cache doesn't help): Settings → Apps → Instagram → Storage → Clear Data
  5. Update app: Play Store → Profile → Manage apps & device → Update Instagram
  6. Update Android: Settings → System → Software Update
  7. Update Google Play Services: Play Store → search "Google Play Services" → Update
  8. Check storage: Settings → Storage — ensure at least 2GB free
  9. Fix date/time: Settings → System → Date & time → Automatic ON
  10. Disable VPN: Settings → Network & Internet → VPN → disconnect
  11. Uninstall and reinstall: Long-press Instagram → Uninstall → reinstall from Play Store

Additional Android tips:

  • On Samsung devices, the path may be Settings → Apps → Instagram → Storage
  • On some Android phones, go to Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Instagram
  • Make sure Google Play Services is updated — Instagram relies on it for push notifications and background services
  • If you're using a custom ROM, ensure it's compatible with the latest Instagram version

How to Contact Instagram Support

Method 1 — In-App Reporting

If you can open Instagram on another device or in a browser, report the issue from there:

  1. Go to your profile
  2. Tap the ☰ menu (top right) → Settings and Privacy
  3. Scroll down and tap Help
  4. Select Report a Problem
  5. Tap Report something that isn't working
  6. Describe the issue: "Instagram app won't open / crashes on launch on my [device model]"
  7. Include your device model, OS version, and Instagram app version
  8. Attach a screenshot if possible (of the crash screen or error message)
  9. Tap Submit

Method 2 — Instagram Help Center

Visit help.instagram.com and search for "app won't open" or "app crashing." The Help Center has troubleshooting articles for known issues and bugs. You can also browse the Instagram Community forums where other users share solutions.

Method 3 — Report via Browser

If the app won't open at all, you can report the issue through Instagram's web interface:

  1. Go to instagram.com in your browser
  2. Log in to your account
  3. Click your profile → Settings (gear icon)
  4. Go to Privacy and Security → Support → Report a Problem
  5. Describe your app launch issue in detail and submit

Method 4 — Social Media

For urgent issues, tweet at @Instagram or @InstagramComms on Twitter/X. Include your device model and describe the problem. Be concise and polite — they're more likely to help if your message is clear and specific.

How to Prevent Instagram from Not Opening

  • Enable automatic app updates: Go to App Store/Play Store settings and turn on auto-updates to always have the latest bug-free version
  • Keep your OS updated: Install iOS and Android updates as soon as they're available for best app compatibility
  • Maintain free storage: Keep at least 2–3GB of free space on your device. Delete unused apps, old photos, and large files regularly
  • Restart your device weekly: A regular restart prevents memory leaks and system glitches that can affect app launches
  • Clear cache periodically: On Android, clear Instagram's cache once a month to prevent corruption buildup
  • Don't use modified apps: Instagram++ or other modded versions are unstable, unsupported, and may stop working at any time
  • Disable unnecessary VPNs: Only use a VPN when needed, and disconnect when using Instagram
  • Keep date and time automatic: Always use automatic date/time settings to avoid SSL connection errors
  • Update Google Play Services: On Android, keep Play Services updated for best app compatibility
  • Don't overload your device: Running too many apps simultaneously can cause memory issues that prevent Instagram from launching

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Instagram keep crashing when I try to open it?

App crashes on launch usually mean corrupted app files, low storage, or a bug in the current Instagram version. Start by force-closing the app, restarting your device, and clearing the cache. If that doesn't work, offload the app (iOS) or clear data (Android). The nuclear option is to delete and reinstall Instagram — this fixes the vast majority of crash issues.

Is Instagram down right now?

Check Instagram.com in your browser. If it loads fine, the issue is with your app. If the browser version also fails, Instagram may be experiencing an outage. You can verify at Downdetector or search "Instagram down" on Twitter/X.

Will reinstalling Instagram delete my account?

No — your account, photos, followers, and messages are stored on Instagram's servers, not on your phone. Reinstalling only removes the app files from your device. After reinstalling, log in with your username and password. Everything will be exactly as you left it. The only thing you'll lose is your app login session, so you'll need to sign in again.

Does VPN affect Instagram from opening?

Yes. Some VPNs interfere with Instagram's connection during app launch, causing it to hang or crash. Try disconnecting your VPN and relaunching Instagram. If it works without the VPN, switch to a different server location or use a more reliable VPN provider. Free VPNs are especially prone to causing issues.

Instagram opens but immediately closes — what do I do?

This is a classic sign of app corruption. On iPhone, offload the app via Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Instagram → Offload App, then reinstall. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → Instagram → Storage → Clear Data, then reopen the app. If neither works, delete and reinstall Instagram completely.

Why does Instagram show a black screen when I open it?

A black screen on launch is usually caused by corrupted cache data or a display/GPU issue. Try force-closing the app, restarting your device, and clearing the cache. If you're on an older device, it may struggle with the latest Instagram version — check if your device meets Instagram's minimum requirements. Updating your device's OS can also help.

Instagram was working fine yesterday but won't open today — what changed?

This usually means an automatic update introduced a bug, or Instagram pushed a server-side change. Check if an Instagram update was recently installed — if so, you may need to wait for a hotfix. You can also try clearing cache, offloading the app, or temporarily using Instagram.com in your browser until the issue is resolved.
