Phone Water Damage? Emergency Recovery Guide (2026)

📅 Updated March 2026 ⏱️ 10 min read 💧 Emergency Guide

Dropped your phone in water? Act fast! Water damage is one of the most common phone emergencies, but quick action can save your device. This guide covers exactly what to do in the first minutes, hours, and days after water exposure to maximize your phone's chances of survival.

🚨 DO THIS IMMEDIATELY (First 60 Seconds)

  1. Get it out of the water NOW - Every second counts
  2. Turn it OFF immediately - Don't check if it still works
  3. Remove the case - Water gets trapped underneath
  4. Take out SIM card tray - Creates an exit for water
  5. Wipe with absorbent cloth - Remove surface water
  6. DO NOT plug it in - Electricity + water = damage

What NOT to Do (Common Myths)

❌ DON'T: Use a Hair Dryer

Heat can damage internal components, melt adhesives, and push water deeper into the phone. The forced air can also spread water to previously dry areas.

❌ DON'T: Shake Your Phone

Shaking can force water deeper into the device and spread it to components that weren't initially wet. Let gravity work for you, not against you.

❌ DON'T: Press Any Buttons

Pressing buttons can push water into the internal circuitry through button gaps. Avoid all buttons until the phone is completely dry.

❌ DON'T: Charge Your Phone

Never charge a wet phone. This can cause short circuits, permanent damage, or even fire. Wait at least 48 hours before attempting to charge.

Step-by-Step Recovery Process

1

Immediate Actions (0-5 minutes)

Turn off the phone completely. Remove case, SIM tray, and any accessories. Wipe exterior with microfiber cloth. Keep the phone upright to let water drain out through charging port.

2

Water Extraction (5-30 minutes)

Gently tap the phone against your hand with charging port facing down. Use a vacuum cleaner (hold at a distance) to draw water out. DO NOT use compressed air - it's too forceful.

3

Drying Phase (24-48 hours)

Place phone in a dry, well-ventilated area. Use silica gel packets if available (much better than rice). Leave SIM tray out. Point a fan at the phone for gentle air circulation.

4

Testing (After 48 hours)

Only after 48+ hours of drying, try turning on the phone. Check all functions: screen, speakers, microphone, camera, charging. If issues persist, seek professional repair.

Rice vs Silica Gel: What Actually Works

⚠️ The Truth About Rice: Rice is not very effective at absorbing moisture from phones. It can also leave starchy residue in ports. If you have nothing else, it's better than nothing, but silica gel is far superior.

✅ Best Drying Agents (Ranked)

  1. Silica gel packets - Most effective, reusable if dried
  2. Calcium chloride - Very effective moisture absorber
  3. Uncooked rice - Okay in emergencies only
  4. Cat litter (crystal type) - Similar to silica gel
  5. Just air drying - Better than wrong methods

IP Ratings: What They Mean

Modern phones have IP (Ingress Protection) ratings that indicate water resistance:

  • IP67: Can survive submersion in 1 meter of water for 30 minutes
  • IP68: Can survive submersion deeper than 1 meter (varies by manufacturer)
  • IPX7/IPX8: Water resistant but not dust resistant
💡 Important: Water resistance is not permanent. It degrades over time and can be compromised by drops, repairs, or wear. Never intentionally submerge your phone.

Signs of Water Damage

  • Discolored or foggy screen
  • Muffled or no sound from speakers
  • Camera lens fog
  • Charging issues or won't charge
  • Random restarts or crashes
  • Unresponsive touchscreen
  • Water damage indicator triggered (usually in SIM tray)

When to Seek Professional Help

  • Phone won't turn on after 48 hours of drying
  • Screen shows visible water damage or discoloration
  • Phone was submerged in salt water, chlorine, or other liquids
  • You see corrosion around ports or buttons
  • Important data needs recovery

  • Replace if: Phone was submerged for extended time, shows multiple failures, or is 4+ years old
⚠️ Liquid Damage Warranty: Water damage is typically not covered by standard warranties, even for phones with IP ratings. Check if you have insurance or AppleCare+ that covers accidental damage.

Prevention Tips

  • Use a waterproof case near water (pools, beaches, boats)
  • Keep phone in sealed bag at the beach
  • Don't use phone in the bathroom (steam can damage it)
  • Be careful at bars and restaurants (spilled drinks)
  • Consider phone insurance that covers water damage
  • Back up your data regularly
