PayPal Not Working? How to Fix It

1. Check PayPal Server Status

Before troubleshooting your device, confirm that PayPal itself is up and running.

How to check PayPal status

  1. Visit downdetector.com/status/paypal and look for recent spikes in reports.
  2. Check PayPal's official @AskPayPal Twitter/X account for outage announcements.
  3. Try accessing paypal.com from a different network (mobile data vs Wi-Fi).
Tip: If Downdetector shows a major outage, the only fix is to wait. PayPal typically resolves service issues within 1–4 hours.

2. Payment Failing or Declined

Payment failures are one of the most common PayPal problems. Here's a systematic approach:

Step 1: Check your PayPal balance

If your PayPal balance is the funding source, make sure it covers the payment amount including any fees.

Step 2: Verify your linked card/bank

Re-verify a linked card or bank

  1. Log in to paypal.com → click your name → Account Settings → Money, Banks and Cards.
  2. Click on the failing card or bank. If it shows "Unconfirmed", follow the steps to confirm it.
  3. For cards: PayPal sends a small test charge — enter the exact amount to verify.
  4. For banks: PayPal sends two micro-deposits within 1–2 business days.

Step 3: Contact your bank

Your bank or card issuer may be blocking the PayPal charge as a security precaution. Call the number on the back of your card and ask them to allow PayPal transactions.

Step 4: Change your funding source

During checkout, click "Change" next to the payment method and select a different card, bank, or your PayPal balance. If one source fails, another may work.

3. Account Limited or Restricted

PayPal may limit your account for a variety of reasons — most can be resolved quickly.

Resolve a PayPal limitation

  1. Log in to paypal.com. If your account is limited, you'll see a yellow banner at the top.
  2. Click "Get Started" or "Resolve" next to the limitation.
  3. Follow the on-screen steps — typically: verify your identity (passport/ID), confirm your bank account, or provide business information.
  4. Upload any requested documents in a clear, unobscured photo or PDF.
  5. Allow PayPal up to 3–5 business days to review your submission.
Warning: If you received an email claiming your account is limited, access PayPal only by typing paypal.com directly — never click links in suspicious emails (phishing).

4. Transfer Stuck or Pending

Standard bank transfer (1–3 business days)

Standard transfers to linked bank accounts take 1–3 US/UK business days. This is normal and does not indicate a problem.

Instant transfer not arriving

If an instant transfer is delayed

  1. Check your PayPal Activity log — if it shows "Completed", the funds have left PayPal and your bank may be processing them.
  2. Confirm your debit card is eligible for instant transfers (most Visa and Mastercard debit cards are).
  3. Contact your bank if the funds haven't appeared within 30 minutes of "Completed" status.

5. Can't Log In

Fix PayPal login problems

  1. Click "Having trouble logging in?" on the PayPal login page.
  2. Enter your email and choose to reset via email link or SMS code.
  3. If 2FA is the issue: check your authenticator app is time-synced, or use backup codes.
  4. If your account is locked after too many failed attempts, wait 30 minutes or use the reset flow.
  5. Check whether you have multiple PayPal accounts linked to different email addresses.

6. PayPal App Crashing or Not Loading

For iPhone (iOS)

  1. Force-close PayPal: swipe up from the bottom → swipe away the PayPal card.
  2. Check for an app update: App Store → Search → PayPal → Update if available.
  3. Free up storage: Settings → General → iPhone Storage — if under 500 MB free, delete unused apps.
  4. Reinstall PayPal: hold the app icon → Remove App → reinstall from the App Store.

For Android

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → PayPal → Storage → Clear Cache (then try; if still failing, Clear Data).
  2. Update PayPal in the Play Store.
  3. Check that your Android version is 8.0 or higher (PayPal minimum requirement).
  4. Uninstall and reinstall PayPal from the Play Store.

7. PayPal Not Working in Browser

  1. Clear your browser cache and cookies: Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → All time.
  2. Disable browser extensions, especially ad blockers (uBlock, AdBlock) — they can interfere with PayPal's checkout scripts.
  3. Try PayPal in a private/incognito window to isolate extension conflicts.
  4. Switch to a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) to rule out a browser-specific bug.
  5. Disable VPN or proxy — PayPal may block connections that appear to come from unexpected locations.

8. Contact PayPal Support

If none of the above fixes your issue:

  • Live Chat: paypal.com/help → click the chat icon (fastest option, typically <5 min wait).
  • Phone: 1-888-221-1161 (US). Available Mon–Fri 5am–10pm, weekends 8am–8pm PT.
  • Community Forum: paypal-community.com — search for your error message before posting.
  • Resolution Centre: For disputes about transactions, use paypal.com/resolutioncenter.
