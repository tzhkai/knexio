Forgot Password? How to Recover Your Accounts

Forgot your password? Don't panic. This guide covers password recovery for Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, Apple ID, Microsoft, and Twitter/X.
Security Warning: Never share your passwords or recovery codes with anyone. Legitimate companies will never ask for your password via email or phone.

General Tips

  • Always use the official website or app for password recovery
  • Have access to your recovery email or phone number
  • Be prepared to answer security questions
  • Use a strong, unique password when resetting
  • Enable two-factor authentication after recovery

Gmail / Google Account

G Google Account Recovery

  1. Go to Google Account Recovery
  2. Enter your Gmail address and click "Next"
  3. Click "Forgot password?"
  4. Google will ask you to enter the last password you remember
  5. Verify your identity using recovery email, phone, or security questions
  6. Create a new strong password
  7. Sign in with your new password

Facebook

f Facebook Password Reset

  1. Go to Facebook Find Your Account
  2. Enter your email, phone number, or full name
  3. Click "Search" and select your account
  4. Choose how to receive the reset code (Email or SMS)
  5. Enter the 6-digit security code
  6. Create a new password
  7. Choose to log out of all devices (recommended)

Instagram

📷 Instagram Password Reset

  1. Open Instagram app or go to Instagram Password Reset
  2. Tap "Forgot password?"
  3. Enter your username, email, or phone number
  4. Choose to receive the reset link via email or SMS
  5. Click the link in your email or SMS
  6. Create a new password

Apple ID

🍎 Apple ID Password Reset

  1. Go to iforgot.apple.com
  2. Enter your Apple ID (email address)
  3. Choose verification method: Email, Security Questions, or Two-Factor Authentication
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions
  5. Create a new password (must be at least 8 characters)

Microsoft Account

⊞ Microsoft Password Reset

  1. Go to Microsoft Password Reset
  2. Enter your Microsoft account email
  3. Complete the security check
  4. Choose verification method: Email, SMS, or Authenticator app
  5. Enter the verification code
  6. Create a new password

Twitter / X

𝕏 Twitter Password Reset

  1. Go to Twitter Password Reset
  2. Enter your email, phone number, or username
  3. Click "Search" and select your account
  4. Choose how to receive the reset code
  5. Enter the verification code
  6. Create a new password

What If You Can't Recover Your Account?

  • No access to recovery email/phone: Contact the platform's support team directly
  • Account hacked: Report it as compromised during the recovery process
  • Account disabled: Follow the platform's appeal process
  • Two-factor authentication issues: Use backup codes or contact support

Create Strong Passwords

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Avoid common words, names, or dates
  • Use a unique password for each account
  • Consider using a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, iCloud Keychain)
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) when available
