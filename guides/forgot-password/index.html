Forgot Password? How to Recover Your Accounts (2026)

📅 Updated March 2026 ⏱️ 20 min read 🔐 6 Major Services

Forgot your password? Don't panic. This comprehensive guide will walk you through password recovery for the most popular online services including Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, Apple ID, Microsoft, and Twitter/X. Follow our step-by-step instructions to regain access to your accounts.

⚠️ Security Warning: Never share your passwords or recovery codes with anyone. Legitimate companies will never ask for your password via email or phone.

General Password Recovery Tips

  • Always use the official website or app for password recovery
  • Have access to your recovery email or phone number
  • Be prepared to answer security questions
  • Use a strong, unique password when resetting
  • Enable two-factor authentication after recovery
G

Gmail / Google Account

accounts.google.com/signin/recovery

  1. Go to Google Account Recovery
  2. Enter your Gmail address and click "Next"
  3. Click "Forgot password?"
  4. Google will ask you to enter the last password you remember. Try your best guess or click "Try another way"
  5. Verify your identity using one of these methods:
    • Recovery email address
    • Recovery phone number (SMS or call)
    • Security questions (if set up)
  6. Once verified, create a new strong password
  7. Sign in with your new password
💡 Tip: If you don't have access to your recovery email or phone, Google may ask additional questions about your account activity to verify your identity.
f

Facebook

facebook.com/login/identify

  1. Go to Facebook Find Your Account
  2. Enter your email, phone number, or full name
  3. Click "Search"
  4. Select your account from the results
  5. Choose how you want to receive the reset code:
    • Email
    • SMS to your phone
    • Facebook notification (if logged in elsewhere)
  6. Enter the 6-digit security code
  7. Create a new password
  8. Choose to log out of all devices (recommended)
⚠️ Important: If you suspect your account was hacked, select "Log out of all devices" when resetting your password.
📷

Instagram

instagram.com/accounts/password/reset

  1. Open the Instagram app or go to Instagram Password Reset
  2. Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen
  3. Enter your username, email, or phone number
  4. Tap "Next"
  5. Choose how to receive the reset link:
    • Send login link via email
    • Send login link via SMS
    • Log in with Facebook (if accounts are linked)
  6. Click the link in your email or SMS
  7. Create a new password
💡 Tip: If you created Instagram using Facebook, you can log in with Facebook and reset your password from settings.
🍎

Apple ID

iforgot.apple.com

  1. Go to iforgot.apple.com
  2. Enter your Apple ID (email address)
  3. Enter the characters from the image (CAPTCHA)
  4. Click "Continue"
  5. Choose how to reset your password:
    • Email: Apple will send instructions to your rescue email
    • Security Questions: Answer your security questions
    • Two-Factor Authentication: Use a trusted device or phone number
  6. Follow the on-screen instructions
  7. Create a new password (must be at least 8 characters)
💡 Tip: With two-factor authentication enabled, you can reset your password directly from a trusted iPhone, iPad, or Mac in Settings.
⊞

Microsoft Account

account.live.com/password/reset

  1. Go to Microsoft Password Reset
  2. Enter your Microsoft account email
  3. Enter the characters shown (security check)
  4. Click "Next"
  5. Choose verification method:
    • Email a reset link
    • Text a code to your phone
    • Use authenticator app (if set up)
  6. Enter the verification code
  7. Create a new password
  8. Sign in with your new password
𝕏

Twitter / X

twitter.com/account/begin_password_reset

  1. Go to Twitter Password Reset
  2. Enter your email, phone number, or username
  3. Click "Search"
  4. Select your account
  5. Choose how to receive the reset code
  6. Enter the verification code
  7. Create a new password
  8. Choose whether to log out of all sessions

What If You Can't Recover Your Account?

  • No access to recovery email/phone: Contact the platform's support team directly
  • Account hacked: Report it as compromised during the recovery process
  • Account disabled: Follow the platform's appeal process
  • Two-factor authentication issues: Use backup codes or contact support

🔐 Create a Strong Password

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Avoid common words, names, or dates
  • Use a unique password for each account
  • Consider using a password manager
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) when available

Password Manager Recommendations

To avoid forgetting passwords in the future, consider using a password manager:

  • 1Password - Best overall, works on all devices
  • Bitwarden - Best free option
  • iCloud Keychain - Best for Apple users
  • Google Password Manager - Built into Chrome and Android
