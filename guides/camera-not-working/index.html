Camera Not Working? Here's How to Fix It

A camera that shows a black screen, says "Camera Unavailable," or won't open is usually a software issue — not a hardware failure. This guide walks you through fixes for iPhone, Android, Windows, and Mac.
Quick fix: Force-close the Camera app and reopen it. If that doesn't work, restart your device — this resolves 70% of camera issues immediately.

Fixes for iPhone

Step 1 — Force Close the Camera App

Swipe up from the bottom (or double-click Home) to see open apps. Swipe the Camera app upward to close it, then reopen.

Step 2 — Restart iPhone

Hold Side + Volume Down → Slide to power off → Wait 30 seconds → Power on again.

Step 3 — Free Up Storage

If storage is full (Settings → General → iPhone Storage), the camera can't save photos and may freeze. Delete unused apps or photos.

Step 4 — Reset All Settings

Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset All Settings. This won't delete your data but resets network, privacy, and display settings.

Fixes for Android

Step 1 — Clear Camera App Cache

Settings → Apps → Camera → Storage → Clear Cache. This removes corrupted temporary data without deleting your photos.

Step 2 — Close Other Apps Using Camera

Apps like Snapchat, Instagram, or video call apps may be holding the camera. Close all background apps first.

Step 3 — Update the Camera App

Open Google Play Store → My apps → Check if Camera or Google Camera has an update available.

Step 4 — Safe Mode Test

Boot into Safe Mode (hold power → long press "Power Off"). If camera works in Safe Mode, a third-party app is interfering.

Fixes for Windows Webcam

Step 1 — Check Camera Privacy Settings

Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Make sure "Camera access" is on, and the specific app (Zoom, Teams, etc.) has permission.

Step 2 — Update Camera Driver

Right-click Start → Device Manager → Cameras → Right-click your camera → Update Driver.

Step 3 — Run the Hardware Troubleshooter

Settings → System → Troubleshoot → Other Troubleshooters → Camera.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my camera showing a black screen?

Another app is likely using the camera. Close all apps, restart your device, and reopen the Camera app.

Camera not working on Zoom or Teams?

Check privacy settings: both Windows and Mac require apps to explicitly have camera permission. Also confirm the correct camera is selected in app settings.
