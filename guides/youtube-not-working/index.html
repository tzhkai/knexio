YouTube Not Working? Here's How to Fix It

YouTube problems range from videos that won't play, black screens, infinite loading, to specific error codes. Most issues are caused by cache problems, outdated apps, or network issues — and are easy to fix in a few steps.
Check for outages first: Visit downdetector.com/status/youtube — if thousands of users are reporting issues, it's a YouTube server problem. Wait it out (usually resolves in under an hour).

Fixes for Browser (youtube.com)

Step 1 — Hard Refresh

Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) to force a hard refresh, bypassing cached content. This fixes stale data causing playback issues.

Step 2 — Disable Ad Blocker

YouTube actively blocks ad blockers and may show a black screen or error when one is detected. Temporarily disable your ad blocker or whitelist youtube.com.

Step 3 — Clear Browser Cache

Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Select "Cached images and files" + "Cookies and other site data" → Clear data → Reload YouTube.

Step 4 — Disable Hardware Acceleration

Chrome: Settings → System → Turn off "Use hardware acceleration when available" → Relaunch. This fixes black screen issues caused by GPU rendering conflicts.

Step 5 — Try Incognito Mode or Different Browser

Press Ctrl+Shift+N (Chrome) to open incognito. If YouTube works there, an extension is the culprit. Try disabling extensions one by one.

Fixes for Android

Step 1 — Clear YouTube Cache

Settings → Apps → YouTube → Storage → Clear Cache. This is the most effective fix for YouTube freezing, buffering, or failing to load on Android.

Step 2 — Update YouTube

Open Play Store → search YouTube → Update if available. Old versions break as YouTube updates its API.

Step 3 — Check Date and Time

Settings → General Management → Date and Time → Enable "Automatic date and time". Incorrect system time causes SSL certificate errors that break YouTube and many other apps.

Fixes for iPhone

Step 1 — Force Quit and Reopen

Swipe up from bottom → swipe YouTube card up → reopen the app. This clears the app's in-memory state.

Step 2 — Reinstall YouTube

Delete the YouTube app → reinstall from the App Store. iOS doesn't have a simple cache-clearing option, so reinstalling is the equivalent.

Step 3 — Restart iPhone

Hold power + volume down → Slide to power off → Turn back on. A restart fixes most temporary app glitches.

Fixes for Smart TV / Fire TV

Step 1 — Restart the TV

Unplug the TV from the wall for 60 seconds, then plug back in. This fully clears the TV's memory and resets the network connection.

Step 2 — Clear YouTube App Cache on TV

Settings → Apps → YouTube → Clear cache. Available on Android TV / Google TV and Fire TV.

Step 3 — Reinstall YouTube on TV

Uninstall the YouTube app and reinstall from your TV's app store. Smart TV apps frequently become outdated and need reinstallation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does YouTube keep pausing by itself?

YouTube pauses automatically after a long period of inactivity to save bandwidth. It may also pause due to "Video interrupted by another app" alerts, a flaky internet connection, or the Auto-pause Reminders feature if you have it enabled in YouTube Settings.
