Gmail is used by over 1.8 billion people, and even small disruptions affect millions of users. This guide covers every major Gmail issue — from login problems and emails stuck in outbox to notification failures, sync problems, filter issues, and IMAP configuration.

1. Gmail Login & Authentication Issues

If you cannot sign in to your Gmail account or are repeatedly asked for a password:

How to Fix Login Problems

  1. Check your username carefully — Make sure you're entering the correct email address (e.g., , not just "you").
  2. Use the correct password — Visit accounts.google.com/signin/recovery to reset your password if forgotten.
  3. Enable 2-Step Verification — If you have it enabled, you'll need your phone or backup codes to sign in.
  4. Check if the account was deleted — Google deletes accounts inactive for 2 years. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered.
  5. Clear browser cookies — Old session cookies can cause "wrong password" errors even with the correct credentials.
  6. Check for a work/school account — If using a Google Workspace account, login may be controlled by your organization. Contact your admin.
  7. Try a different device — If 2FA is causing issues, try signing in on a trusted device that was previously logged in.
Security Alert: If you see "Suspicious sign-in prevented," Google blocked a login attempt. Change your password immediately and enable 2-Step Verification. Visit myaccount.google.com/security.

2. Gmail Not Loading

First, confirm whether this is a Gmail-wide outage or an issue on your device only:

  • Visit the Google Workspace Status Dashboard.
  • Check Downdetector for user reports.

If Gmail is fine globally, the issue is local. Try these quick fixes:

Quick Fixes

  1. Refresh the Gmail page (F5 or Ctrl+R).
  2. Check your internet connection by opening another website.
  3. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  4. Try Gmail in an incognito or private browsing window.
  5. Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers).
  6. Try a different browser or device.

3. Emails Not Sending

If emails are stuck in the Outbox or show a "send" error:

  1. Check file attachment size – Gmail has a 25 MB attachment limit. Use Google Drive to share larger files.
  2. Verify recipient address – A typo in the email address causes a delivery failure. Double-check it.
  3. Check internet connection – A dropped connection mid-send leaves emails in Outbox.
  4. Refresh the page – In the Gmail web app, try Ctrl+Shift+U to reload without cache.
  5. Sign out and back in – Authentication tokens can expire. Sign out completely and sign back in.
  6. Check if you're over the sending limit – Free accounts can send ~500 emails per day; Google Workspace has higher limits.
  7. Verify the recipient's email server isn't blocking you – Large attachments or flagged content can trigger spam filters on the recipient's end.
Note: If your Gmail shows "Sending limit exceeded," Google has temporarily blocked outgoing mail. Wait 24-48 hours, or upgrade to Google Workspace for higher limits.

4. Gmail Notifications Not Working

On Android

  1. Settings → Apps → Gmail → Notifications → make sure "Allow Notifications" is on.
  2. Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → find Gmail → set to "Don't optimize".
  3. In Gmail app: Menu (☰) → Settings → your account → Notifications → set to "All".
  4. Check that Do Not Disturb mode is off or Gmail is allowed through it.

On iPhone

  1. Settings → Notifications → Gmail → Allow Notifications → turn on.
  2. Settings → Gmail → Background App Refresh → turn on.
  3. In Gmail app: tap your avatar → Manage your Google Account → Security → check no "Do Not Disturb" is set.
  4. Check Focus/DND settings: if iPhone Focus mode is on, it may block Gmail notifications.

5. Gmail Not Syncing

If new emails don't appear on your device:

  1. Force a manual sync – In the Gmail app, pull down to refresh.
  2. Check sync settings – Gmail app → Menu → Settings → your account → Days of mail to sync → increase the number.
  3. On Android: Settings → Accounts → Google → your account → make sure "Gmail" is checked under account sync.
  4. Make sure Background App Refresh (iOS) or Background Data (Android) is enabled for Gmail.
  5. If using a VPN, try disabling it — some VPNs block Google services.
  6. Check for conflicts — If you have Gmail set up both via the Gmail app and via iOS Mail, they may conflict. Remove one.
  7. Check the sync direction — In Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → IMAP, ensure the correct folders are set to sync.

6. Gmail Storage Full

Google provides 15 GB of free storage shared between Gmail, Drive, and Photos. When full, you cannot receive new emails. To free up space:

  1. In Gmail search: has:attachment larger:5m — find and delete large emails.
  2. In Gmail search: older_than:1y — find old emails you no longer need.
  3. Empty your Trash folder: Settings → Trash → Empty Trash now.
  4. Empty Spam: Settings → Spam → Delete all spam messages.
  5. Delete old automated emails from services like newsletters, order confirmations, etc.
  6. Check Google Drive and Google Photos for large unused files at one.google.com/storage.
  7. Unsubscribe from newsletters — Search unsubscribe in Gmail to find marketing emails and unsubscribe in bulk.
Tip: Use Google One to upgrade to 100 GB storage for $1.99/month if you regularly hit the free limit. Your storage is shared across Gmail, Drive, and Photos.
Warning: Deleting emails from Gmail permanently removes them from all synced devices. Make sure you don't need an email before deleting it.

7. Gmail Filter & Rule Problems

If your Gmail filters (also called "rules") are not working correctly:

Common Filter Issues and Fixes

  1. Filter not applying to existing emails — Filters only apply to NEW emails by default. To apply to existing emails, edit the filter and check "Also apply filter to matching conversations."
  2. Filter conflicts — If two filters contradict each other, the most recently created one wins. Review all filters for conflicts.
  3. Nested label actions may fail — If your filter applies a nested label (e.g., Work/Clients), try using a top-level label instead.
  4. Too many filters — Gmail has a limit of ~1,000 filters per account. Having hundreds of filters can slow processing.
  5. Filter action "Skip Inbox" not working — Check if another filter with higher priority is archiving or moving the same emails.
  6. Auto-forwarding stopped — If emails are no longer being forwarded, verify the forwarding address is still verified in Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.

How to Check and Edit Your Filters

  1. Open Gmail → click the Settings gear icon → See all settings.
  2. Go to the Filters tab at the top.
  3. Review all active filters. Click "Edit" or "Delete" next to any filter.
  4. Make sure the filter criteria match the emails you're trying to process.
  5. Click "Validate" or manually apply the filter by selecting matching emails and choosing "Filter messages like these."
Tip: To find emails that aren't being filtered correctly, use Gmail search with the criteria your filter uses. This shows you if matching emails exist and where they're ending up.

8. Gmail Not Working on iPhone or Android

iPhone Fixes

  1. Force-close Gmail and reopen.
  2. Update Gmail from the App Store.
  3. Update iOS: Settings → General → Software Update.
  4. Delete and reinstall Gmail.
  5. Re-add your Google account: Settings → Mail (or Passwords & Accounts) → remove Gmail → add again.
  6. Check Screen Time restrictions — Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → ensure Gmail is allowed.
  7. Reset notifications — Settings → Notifications → Gmail → Reset Notifications.

Android Fixes

  1. Clear Gmail cache: Settings → Apps → Gmail → Storage → Clear Cache.
  2. Clear data (this signs you out): Settings → Apps → Gmail → Storage → Clear Data → then sign back in.
  3. Update Gmail from Google Play.
  4. Check Google Play Services is up to date (required for Gmail sync).
  5. Uninstall Gmail updates and reinstall (Gmail is a system app on most Android devices).
  6. Check app permissions — Settings → Apps → Gmail → Permissions → ensure Storage, SMS, and Notifications are allowed.

9. Gmail Not Working in Browser

  1. Use Basic HTML view if Gmail fails to load: scroll to the bottom of the Gmail login page and click "Load basic HTML".
  2. Clear cache: Chrome → Ctrl+Shift+Delete → check "Cached images and files" → Clear data.
  3. Disable extensions: try Chrome Incognito mode (Ctrl+Shift+N).
  4. Change DNS to 8.8.8.8 (Google DNS) or 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare).
  5. Use the Gmail web app instead of a desktop email client if the client fails to connect.
  6. Check firewall/proxy settings — corporate networks often block Gmail.

10. Gmail IMAP & POP Settings

If you're setting up Gmail in a third-party email client (Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird, etc.):

IMAP Settings (Recommended)

  • IMAP Server: imap.gmail.com
  • Port: 993 (SSL required)
  • Username: Your full Gmail address ()
  • Password: Your Gmail password OR an App Password (required if 2FA is on)

POP Settings

  • POP Server: pop.gmail.com
  • Port: 995 (SSL required)
  • Username: Your full Gmail address
  • Password: Your Gmail password OR an App Password
  • POP download: Choose "All Mail" or "Mail from now on" depending on your needs.

SMTP Settings (for sending)

  • SMTP Server: smtp.gmail.com
  • Port: 587 (TLS/STARTTLS) or 465 (SSL)
  • Username: Your full Gmail address
  • Password: App Password (required if 2FA is on)
Important: Before using IMAP/POP, enable access in Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Enable IMAP or Enable POP.

How to Enable 2FA and Get an App Password

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Click 2-Step Verification → follow the prompts to enable it.
  3. Once 2FA is enabled, go to App passwords.
  4. Select "Mail" and your device type → click Generate.
  5. Copy the 16-character password and use it in your email client's SMTP/IMAP settings.
