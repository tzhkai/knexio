YouTube App Not Working? 15 Ways to Fix Common YouTube Problems

Updated March 2026 · 8 min read

Quick Fixes

  • Check internet connection
  • Restart YouTube app
  • Clear app cache
  • Update YouTube app
  • Restart your device

YouTube is the world's largest video platform. When the app stops working, it can be frustrating. This guide covers solutions for crashes, buffering, and playback issues on iPhone, Android, and smart TVs.

Common YouTube Problems

  • YouTube app won't open or crashes on launch
  • Videos won't play or keep buffering
  • Black screen when playing videos
  • "Connection to server lost" errors
  • YouTube stuck on loading screen
  • Audio not playing with video
  • Comments won't load
  • YouTube not working on smart TV
  • "An error occurred" messages

1. Check YouTube Server Status

Before troubleshooting, verify if YouTube is experiencing a global outage:

  • Check Downdetector for YouTube outage reports
  • Search "YouTube down" on Twitter/X for real-time reports
  • Try accessing YouTube on a different device

If YouTube servers are down, you'll need to wait for Google to fix the issue.

2. Check Your Internet Connection

YouTube requires a stable internet connection:

  1. Turn off Wi-Fi and try mobile data (or vice versa)
  2. Run a speed test - YouTube needs at least 3 Mbps for 720p, 5 Mbps for 1080p
  3. Restart your router if on Wi-Fi
  4. Check if other apps can access the internet
  5. Disable VPN (YouTube may block some VPNs)

3. Force Close and Restart YouTube

iPhone/iPad

  1. Swipe up from the bottom (or double-click Home button on older devices)
  2. Find YouTube in the app switcher
  3. Swipe up on YouTube to close it
  4. Reopen YouTube from your home screen

Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → YouTube
  2. Tap "Force Stop"
  3. Reopen YouTube

4. Restart Your Device

  1. Power off your device completely
  2. Wait 10-15 seconds
  3. Power it back on
  4. Try opening YouTube again

5. Update YouTube App

iPhone/iPad

  1. Open App Store
  2. Search for "YouTube"
  3. If "Update" is available, tap it
  4. Wait for the update to complete

Android

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Search for "YouTube"
  3. Tap "Update" if available
  4. Or enable auto-updates for all apps

6. Clear YouTube App Cache

iPhone/iPad

iPhone doesn't allow clearing individual app cache. Try:

  1. Offload the YouTube app: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → YouTube → Offload App
  2. Reinstall YouTube (this preserves your data)

Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → YouTube
  2. Tap "Storage & cache"
  3. Tap "Clear cache" (don't clear data)
  4. Restart YouTube

7. Fix Videos Won't Play or Buffering Issues

  1. Check your internet speed (minimum 3 Mbps for 720p)
  2. Lower video quality: Tap gear icon → Quality → Select 480p or 720p
  3. Close other apps using bandwidth
  4. Restart your router
  5. Try a different video to see if the issue persists
  6. Disable any ad blockers if installed
Tip: Auto-play may cause buffering. Disable it in Settings → Autoplay.

8. Fix Black Screen Issue

When you hear audio but see a black screen:

  1. Tap the screen to bring up controls
  2. Check if "Quality" is set too high - lower it
  3. Try switching to full-screen mode
  4. Update your device's operating system
  5. Update graphics drivers (on computers)
  6. Disable hardware acceleration in browser (if using web)

9. Fix YouTube App Crashing

  1. Check available storage - ensure at least 1GB free
  2. Clear app cache (see Solution 6)
  3. Update YouTube and your device's OS
  4. Disable VPN if active
  5. Check if YouTube is down (see Solution 1)
  6. Reinstall YouTube app as last resort

10. Fix YouTube on Smart TV

  1. Update your TV's firmware (Settings → System → Software Update)
  2. Sign out and sign back into YouTube
  3. Uninstall and reinstall YouTube app
  4. Restart your smart TV
  5. Check your TV's internet connection
  6. Use a different HDMI port if using a streaming stick
  7. Try Chromecast or AirPlay as alternative

11. Check Date and Time Settings

Incorrect date/time can cause connection errors:

  1. Open Settings on your device
  2. Go to General → Date & Time (iOS) or System → Date & Time (Android)
  3. Enable "Set Automatically"
  4. Restart YouTube

12. Disable VPN and Proxy

YouTube may block or limit VPN connections:

  1. Disable VPN in your VPN app
  2. Disable proxy in Settings → Wi-Fi → (i) → HTTP Proxy → Off
  3. Restart YouTube
  4. Try accessing YouTube again
Tip: If you must use a VPN, try different servers or use a paid VPN service with better YouTube support.

13. Check for Conflicting Apps

Other apps can interfere with YouTube:

  • Screen recording apps (OBS, AZ Recorder)
  • Ad blockers (AdGuard, Block This)
  • Download managers
  • System optimizers

Temporarily disable these apps to see if YouTube works.

14. Reinstall YouTube App

⚠️ Note: Reinstalling won't delete your watch history or subscriptions as these are linked to your Google account.

iPhone/iPad

  1. Long-press YouTube app icon
  2. Tap "Remove App" → Delete
  3. Restart your device
  4. Download YouTube from App Store
  5. Sign in with your Google account

Android

  1. Open Settings → Apps → YouTube
  2. Tap "Uninstall"
  3. Restart your device
  4. Download YouTube from Google Play Store
  5. Sign in with your Google account

15. Update Device Operating System

iPhone/iPad

  1. Settings → General → Software Update
  2. Download and install any available updates
  3. Restart your device after updating

Android

  1. Settings → System → System Update
  2. Check for updates
  3. Download and install
  4. Restart your device

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is YouTube not working today?

YouTube may not be working due to server outages, internet connection issues, app bugs, or device problems. Check YouTube's server status first using Downdetector before troubleshooting your device.

How do I fix YouTube buffering?

To fix buffering issues:

  1. Lower video quality (720p or 480p)
  2. Close other apps using bandwidth
  3. Restart your router
  4. Check your internet speed (minimum 3 Mbps for 720p)
  5. Disable auto-play
  6. Try a different video to rule out server issues

Why does YouTube show a black screen?

Black screen issues are usually caused by:

  • Video quality set too high for your connection
  • Outdated app or device OS
  • Hardware acceleration issues
  • Corrupted app cache

Try lowering video quality, updating the app, or clearing cache.

Why won't YouTube load videos?

Videos won't load if:

  • Your internet connection is unstable
  • YouTube servers are down
  • Your app is outdated
  • You have insufficient storage
  • A VPN or proxy is blocking connection

Check your connection, update the app, and disable any VPN.

How much data does YouTube use?

YouTube data usage varies by quality:

  • 480p: ~300MB per hour
  • 720p: ~900MB per hour
  • 1080p: ~1.5GB per hour
  • 4K: ~6-7GB per hour

Lower the quality to save data on mobile plans.

Why is YouTube app crashing on my phone?

YouTube crashes are caused by:

  • Corrupted cache files
  • Insufficient storage space
  • Outdated app version
  • Device compatibility issues
  • Conflicting apps

Clear cache, update the app, or reinstall YouTube.

How do I fix YouTube not working on smart TV?

For smart TV issues:

  1. Update your TV's firmware
  2. Restart your TV
  3. Sign out and sign back into YouTube
  4. Reinstall YouTube app
  5. Check your TV's internet connection
  6. Try using Chromecast or AirPlay as alternative

Can I use YouTube without the app?

Yes! You can:

  • Use YouTube at youtube.com in any browser
  • Use YouTube Music app for music
  • Use YouTube TV app for live TV (subscription required)
  • Use m.youtube.com for mobile-optimized web experience

Why does YouTube say "an error occurred"?

This error typically means:

  • The video is unavailable in your region
  • The video has been removed or is private
  • Your connection is unstable
  • YouTube servers are experiencing issues

Try a different video or check your connection.

How do I enable dark mode on YouTube?

Mobile app:

  1. Tap your profile picture
  2. Tap "Settings"
  3. Tap "General"
  4. Enable "Dark theme"

Why is YouTube audio not working?

Audio issues are usually fixed by:

  1. Checking if device volume is up
  2. Checking if video volume is up (tap speaker icon)
  3. Restarting the video
  4. Restarting the app
  5. Checking if headphones are connected

How do I download YouTube videos for offline viewing?

YouTube Premium subscribers can:

  1. Open the video
  2. Tap "Download" below the video
  3. Choose quality
  4. Access downloads in Library → Downloads
Tip: Downloads are available offline for up to 30 days.
