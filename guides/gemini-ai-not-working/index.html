Gemini AI Not Working? Try These Fixes

If Google Gemini is not loading, showing error messages, or not responding to your prompts, this guide explains the most common causes and how to fix them on any device.
Quick check: Visit Google Workspace Status to see if Google's AI services are experiencing an outage.

Why Gemini AI Is Not Working

  • Google server outage or scheduled maintenance
  • Outdated browser or corrupted cache data
  • Signed out of your Google account during the session
  • Ad blockers or browser extensions interfering with Gemini
  • VPN routing traffic through a region where Gemini is unavailable
  • Gemini not available in your country or region
  • Google account flagged or restricted
  • Gemini Advanced subscription payment failed or expired

Service Status Checking

Check these official status pages to determine if the issue is on Google's end:

  • Visit workspace.google.com/status for Google Workspace and AI service uptime
  • Check status.cloud.google.com for Google Cloud Platform incidents that may affect Gemini
  • Follow @GoogleWorkspace on X/Twitter for outage announcements
  • If all status pages show green, the issue is likely on your end — proceed with the troubleshooting steps below

Account and Subscription Issues

If you can access Gemini but encounter errors or feature restrictions:

  • Not signed in: Gemini requires an active Google account. Visit myaccount.google.com to confirm you are logged in
  • Age restrictions: Users under 18 cannot access Gemini in most regions due to safety policies
  • Account flagged: If Google detects policy violations or suspicious activity, your account may be temporarily restricted. Check your email for Google account notifications
  • Gemini Advanced expired: Google One AI Premium ($19.99/month) includes Gemini Advanced. Check your subscription at one.google.com
  • Google Workspace restrictions: Some organization-managed accounts disable Gemini by default. Contact your IT administrator if you are on a corporate or school account
  • Location mismatch: If your Google account region does not match your actual location (due to VPN use), Gemini may be unavailable

Rate Limit Explanations

Understanding Gemini's usage limits helps avoid unexpected interruptions:

  • Free tier: Gemini has generous but not unlimited daily limits. Heavy usage may trigger temporary slowdowns or "try again later" messages
  • Gemini Advanced: Included in Google One AI Premium ($19.99/month), this tier offers higher rate limits and priority access to Gemini Ultra, Google's most powerful model
  • Context limits: Each conversation has a context window. Extremely long conversations may require starting a new chat
  • API vs. web: The Gemini API (for developers) has separate rate limits based on your Google Cloud billing tier
  • Rate limit reset: Web tier limits typically reset every 24 hours. If you hit a limit, the error message will usually indicate when to try again

How to Fix Gemini AI

Step 1 — Confirm Your Google Account Is Signed In

Gemini requires an active Google account. Go to myaccount.google.com and confirm you're signed in. If your session expired, sign in again and reload Gemini.

Step 2 — Clear Cache and Cookies

Open Chrome settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data. Select Cached images and Cookies for the past 7 days, then reload gemini.google.com.

Step 3 — Disable Extensions

Open Chrome in incognito mode (Ctrl+Shift+N). If Gemini works there, a browser extension is blocking it. Common culprits include ad blockers, VPN extensions, and content blockers. Disable them for gemini.google.com.

Step 4 — Check Regional Availability

Gemini is not available in all countries. If you are using a VPN, try disabling it — some VPN exit nodes are in restricted regions. If Gemini is not available in your country, consider using ChatGPT or Claude as alternatives.

Step 5 — Update or Switch Your Browser

Gemini works best on the latest version of Chrome. Check for browser updates at chrome://settings/help. If you use another browser, try Chrome or Firefox instead.

Step 6 — Use the Gemini Mobile App

Download the Gemini app for iOS or Android. The app bypasses browser-related issues and provides a more stable experience.

Step 7 — Sign Out and Sign Back In

Click your profile icon → Sign out, then sign in again. An expired session token or corrupted authentication data can cause persistent errors.

Browser Troubleshooting

If Gemini loads but behaves incorrectly or shows errors:

  • Browser compatibility: Gemini is optimized for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Internet Explorer and very old browser versions are not supported
  • Disable extensions: Privacy extensions (Ghostery, Privacy Badger) and script blockers can interfere. Temporarily disable all extensions or whitelist gemini.google.com
  • Clear site-specific data: In Chrome, click the lock icon in the address bar → Site settings → Clear data
  • Enable cookies: Gemini requires cookies. Ensure your browser is not blocking cookies for Google domains
  • Disable hardware acceleration: Some users fix glitches by turning this off in browser settings under System or Performance
  • JavaScript enabled: Verify JavaScript is enabled in your browser settings
  • Try a different browser: If Chrome fails, try Firefox or Safari to isolate browser-specific bugs

Alternative Access Methods

If the web interface is unavailable, try these alternatives:

  • Gemini mobile apps: Download for iOS or Android. The apps often work when the web version has issues
  • Gemini API: For developers, use the Gemini API via Google AI Studio or Vertex AI. API endpoints are more stable and have higher rate limits
  • Google Workspace integration: If you have Google Workspace, access Gemini directly in Gmail, Docs, and Sheets via the side panel
  • Gemini in Android: Many newer Android phones have Gemini built into the Google Assistant. Try saying "Hey Google" or long-pressing the home button
  • Alternative AIs: While troubleshooting Gemini, use ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft Copilot

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Google Gemini not working?

Gemini may not work due to Google server issues, browser cache problems, a signed-out Google account, or regional availability limitations.

Is Gemini available in all countries?

No. Gemini is not available in all regions. If you are in a restricted country, disabling your VPN or using an alternative like ChatGPT is recommended.

Why does Gemini keep giving an error?

Persistent errors are usually caused by an outdated browser, cached data, or a Google account session issue. Clearing cache and signing back in usually resolves it.

Is Gemini Advanced worth it?

Gemini Advanced (included in Google One AI Premium) gives access to Gemini Ultra and integration with Gmail and Docs. It costs $19.99/month and is a good choice for heavy Google Workspace users.

How do I check my Gemini rate limit status?

Gemini does not show exact usage numbers, but "try again later" messages indicate you have hit a temporary limit. Limits reset every 24 hours.
