iPhone Won't Turn On? Here's How to Fix It

An iPhone that won't turn on is usually suffering from a dead battery, a software freeze, or a failed update — not a hardware failure. Follow these steps from simplest to most advanced.
Start here: Plug your iPhone into a charger for at least 15-30 minutes. If the battery is completely dead, it needs some charge before the screen will respond.

Step-by-Step Fixes

Step 1 — Charge Your iPhone

Use the original Apple cable and a wall adapter (not a computer USB port, which delivers less power). After 15 minutes, look for the "charging" icon. If you see nothing, try a different cable.

Step 2 — Force Restart (iPhone 8 and later, including all Face ID models)

1. Press and quickly release Volume Up
2. Press and quickly release Volume Down
3. Press and hold the Side button until the Apple logo appears (about 10 seconds)

Step 2 (Alternate) — Force Restart iPhone 7

Hold Volume Down + Sleep/Wake simultaneously until Apple logo appears.

Step 2 (Alternate) — Force Restart iPhone 6s

Hold Home + Sleep/Wake simultaneously for 10 seconds.

Step 3 — Put iPhone in Recovery Mode

Connect iPhone to Mac/PC with Finder or iTunes open. Perform the force restart sequence above but keep holding the Side button even after the Apple logo — hold until you see the "Connect to iTunes/Finder" screen. Click Update first (this preserves data). If Update fails, click Restore.

Step 4 — DFU Mode (Last Software Resort)

DFU (Device Firmware Update) mode bypasses iOS entirely:
1. Connect to computer with Finder/iTunes
2. Press-release Volume Up → press-release Volume Down → hold Side button 3 seconds → while still holding Side, also hold Volume Down for 10 seconds → release Side button, keep holding Volume Down for 5 more seconds
iPhone screen stays black. Finder/iTunes shows "iPhone in Recovery Mode." Click Restore.

Step 5 — Hardware Check

If none of the above work, it may be a hardware issue: failed battery, broken logic board, or liquid damage. Visit an Apple Store or authorized repair center.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my iPhone turn on even when charging?

If the battery is completely drained, it can take 10-15 minutes of charging before any screen appears. Use a different cable to rule out a faulty cable.

Will Recovery Mode erase my iPhone?

Choosing "Update" in Recovery Mode preserves your data. Choosing "Restore" erases all data but reinstalls iOS fresh.
