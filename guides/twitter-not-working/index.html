Twitter (rebranded as X) is used by hundreds of millions of people daily. When it stops working — whether it won't load, the feed freezes, or the app crashes — it's frustrating. This guide covers all common Twitter problems and their fixes for 2026.

1. Twitter Not Loading or Showing a Blank Screen

A blank white or black screen usually means Twitter can't connect to its servers, or the app/browser has corrupted cached data.

Quick Fix Steps

  1. Check Downdetector or status.twitterstat.us to confirm Twitter is not down globally.
  2. Turn your phone's Wi-Fi off and back on, or switch to mobile data to test your connection.
  3. Force-close the Twitter/X app and reopen it.
  4. Restart your device completely.
  5. Clear the Twitter app cache (Android) or offload/reinstall (iPhone).
  6. On a browser, open an incognito window and go to twitter.com to rule out browser extensions.
  7. Check your DNS settings — try Google DNS (8.8.8.8) or Cloudflare (1.1.1.1).

Advanced loading fixes

  1. Disable VPN: Some VPN IPs are blocked by Twitter. Try disconnecting.
  2. Check system time: Incorrect date/time can cause SSL errors. Set to automatic.
  3. Try mobile.twitter.com: The mobile web version sometimes works when the app doesn't.
  4. Check for app updates: Outdated apps may have compatibility issues with Twitter's servers.
Tip: If twitter.com loads fine in a browser but the app doesn't work, the issue is the app — try updating or reinstalling it.

2. Feed Not Refreshing or Showing Old Tweets

A stuck "For You" or "Following" timeline is often caused by a bad network request that got cached.

Timeline refresh fixes

  1. Pull down on the timeline to manually trigger a refresh.
  2. Force-close the app and reopen it. This clears in-memory state.
  3. Check your internet speed — Twitter requires a stable connection to stream new content.
  4. Log out and back in to re-establish your session token.
  5. Switch between "For You" and "Following" tabs to force a refresh.
  6. Uninstall and reinstall if the issue persists after the above steps.

Timeline content issues

  1. Missing tweets: The algorithm may not be showing certain content. Try the "Following" tab for chronological order.
  2. Rate limiting: If you've scrolled extensively, you may hit rate limits. Wait a few minutes.
  3. Content filters: Check Settings → Privacy and Safety → Content you see for any active filters.
  4. Muted/blocked accounts: Verify you haven't muted accounts whose tweets you're looking for.

3. Notifications Not Showing

If you're missing likes, mentions, or reply alerts, it's usually a permissions or background refresh issue.

iPhone

  1. Go to Settings → Notifications → Twitter.
  2. Enable Allow Notifications, Alerts, and Sounds.
  3. Ensure Background App Refresh is on: Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Twitter.
  4. Check Focus modes: Ensure Twitter isn't excluded in any active Focus.
  5. In the Twitter app: Settings → Notifications → ensure desired notification types are enabled.

Android

  1. Long-press the Twitter app → App Info → Notifications.
  2. Enable all notification categories.
  3. Check that Battery Optimization isn't restricting Twitter: Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → Twitter → Don't Optimize.
  4. Ensure "Pause app activity if unused" is disabled for Twitter.
  5. In the Twitter app: Settings → Notifications → ensure desired notification types are enabled.

4. DMs Not Sending

Direct message failures are caused by connection issues, rate limits, or recipient privacy settings.

DM troubleshooting

  1. Check your internet connection — DMs need a stable link.
  2. The recipient may have DMs restricted to "People you follow." You can't DM them unless you're mutually connected or they've enabled DMs from everyone.
  3. If you've sent many DMs recently, you may have hit Twitter's DM rate limit. Wait an hour and try again.
  4. Try sending the DM from twitter.com in a browser instead of the app.
  5. Log out and back in to refresh your auth token.
  6. Check if you've been blocked by the recipient — this prevents DMs.

DM media issues

  1. Photos not sending: Check file size (limit is typically 5MB for images).
  2. GIFs not working: Try using Twitter's built-in GIF picker instead of uploading.
  3. Voice messages: Ensure microphone permissions are granted.

5. Can't Tweet or Post

Tweet posting issues

  1. Rate limiting: Twitter limits how many tweets you can post in a timeframe. Wait a few minutes.
  2. Character limit: Ensure your tweet is under 280 characters (or 4,000 for Twitter Blue subscribers).
  3. Media upload fails: Check file size (images max 5MB, videos max 512MB for standard users).
  4. Account restrictions: Check if you've received any violation notices that limit posting.
  5. Draft issues: Try composing a new tweet instead of using a draft.
  6. Thread fails: If a thread won't post, try posting tweets individually.

6. Twitter Search Not Working

Search troubleshooting

  1. Clear search cache: In the app, go to Settings → Privacy and Safety → Clear search history.
  2. Try advanced search: Use search operators like "from:username" or "to:username".
  3. Check search restrictions: Some sensitive content may be filtered by default.
  4. Rate limiting: Excessive searching can trigger temporary limits.
  5. Use web search: Try searching on twitter.com/search if the app search fails.

7. Twitter App Keeps Crashing

Crashes are typically caused by an outdated app, low storage, or corrupted data.

Crash fixes

  1. Update Twitter to the latest version in the App Store or Google Play Store.
  2. Free up storage space — apps crash when the device is nearly full. Aim for at least 500MB free.
  3. Clear app cache on Android: Settings → Apps → Twitter → Storage → Clear Cache.
  4. On iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Twitter → Offload App, then reinstall.
  5. If crashing only happens when you tap a specific tweet type (e.g., a video), it may be a media codec issue — try disabling Auto-Play Videos in Twitter settings.
  6. Restart your device to clear memory issues.

8. Account Locked or Suspended

Account access issues

  1. Locked account: Follow the verification steps sent to your email or phone to unlock.
  2. Suspended account: You'll receive an email explaining the violation. You can appeal the decision.
  3. Age verification: If asked to verify age, provide the requested documentation.
  4. Bot detection: If flagged for automated behavior, verify you're human through the provided method.
  5. Appeal process: Submit an appeal through the form linked in the suspension notice or at help.twitter.com.
Important: Never pay third parties claiming they can restore your account. Only use official Twitter/X channels for appeals.

9. Fixing Twitter on iPhone

iPhone Users: Complete iOS-specific troubleshooting steps.

Step-by-Step iPhone Fix

  1. Force-close Twitter: swipe up from the bottom and swipe the app away.
  2. Check for updates: App Store → profile icon → scroll to see pending updates.
  3. Restart iPhone: hold Side + Volume Down → slide to power off.
  4. Reset network settings if persistent: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset → Reset Network Settings (you'll need to re-enter Wi-Fi passwords).
  5. Offload and reinstall Twitter as a last resort: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Twitter → Offload App.
  6. Check Screen Time restrictions: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  7. Ensure iOS is updated: Settings → General → Software Update.

10. Fixing Twitter on Android

Android Users: Complete Android-specific troubleshooting steps.

Step-by-Step Android Fix

  1. Force stop the app: Settings → Apps → Twitter → Force Stop.
  2. Clear cache: Settings → Apps → Twitter → Storage → Clear Cache.
  3. Clear data (if needed): Settings → Apps → Twitter → Storage → Clear Data (you'll need to log in again).
  4. Update Twitter in the Play Store.
  5. Restart your Android device.
  6. If problems persist: uninstall Twitter, restart, then reinstall from the Play Store.
  7. Disable battery optimization: Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → Twitter → Don't Optimize.
  8. Check Data Saver: Settings → Network → Data Saver → ensure Twitter is unrestricted.

11. Fixing Twitter in a Browser (PC/Mac)

Browser troubleshooting

  1. Open DevTools (F12 → Console) to check for errors if you're technical.
  2. Clear browser cache: Ctrl+Shift+Del (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Del (Mac) → select "Cached images and files" → Clear data.
  3. Disable browser extensions — ad blockers sometimes interfere with Twitter scripts. Test in an incognito window first.
  4. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) to isolate the issue.
  5. Check that JavaScript is enabled: it's required for twitter.com to work.
  6. Flush DNS: ipconfig /flushdns (Windows) or sudo dscacheutil -flushcache (Mac).
  7. Update your browser to the latest version.
  8. Try disabling hardware acceleration in browser settings.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Common Twitter/X questions answered

Why is Twitter / X not loading?

Twitter may not load due to server outages, slow internet, outdated app, corrupted cache, or a DNS problem. Check status.twitterstat.us to see if X is down globally before troubleshooting your device.

Why are my Twitter notifications not showing?

Missing notifications can be caused by disabled push notifications in your phone settings, a background app refresh being turned off, or the app needing an update. Go to Settings > Notifications > Twitter/X and enable all alerts.

Why can't I send DMs on Twitter?

DM failures often occur due to the recipient's privacy settings, a poor internet connection, or an account issue. Try restarting the app, checking your connection, or accessing DMs from twitter.com in a browser.

Why does the Twitter app keep crashing on my phone?

App crashes are usually caused by an outdated version, corrupted cached data, or insufficient device storage. Update Twitter in the App Store or Play Store, clear the app cache, and ensure you have at least 500MB of free storage.

Why can't I see my Twitter timeline?

Timeline issues are often caused by rate limiting, account restrictions, or app cache problems. Try refreshing the feed, clearing the app cache, or logging out and back in.

Why is my Twitter account locked or suspended?

Accounts may be locked for suspicious activity, violating rules, or automated behavior detection. Follow the instructions in the notification to verify your identity or appeal the decision.

How do I fix Twitter on iPhone?

On iPhone: force-close Twitter, restart your device, check for iOS updates, update the Twitter app from App Store, and if needed, offload and reinstall the app from Settings > General > iPhone Storage.

How do I fix Twitter on Android?

On Android: force-stop the app in Settings > Apps > Twitter, clear cache and data, restart your phone, update from Play Store, and ensure the app has necessary permissions.

Why can't I tweet or post on Twitter?

Tweet failures can be caused by rate limiting (posting too frequently), account restrictions, content violations, or technical issues. Wait a few minutes and try again, or check if you've received any violation notices.

Why is Twitter search not working?

Search issues may be due to temporary server problems, search rate limits, or app cache issues. Try clearing the app cache, using advanced search operators, or accessing search from the web version.

Still not working? If none of the above fixes work, Twitter itself may be down or experiencing issues in your region. Check Downdetector and wait for a service restoration.
