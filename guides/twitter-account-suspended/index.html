Twitter Account Suspended? Here's What to Do

Twitter (now X) suspends accounts for policy violations, spam detection, or security triggers. There are two types: temporary locks that require identity verification, and full suspensions requiring an appeal. Here's how to handle both.
Locked vs Suspended: A locked account just needs phone verification to unlock. A suspended account shows "Your account has been suspended" and requires a formal appeal.

Step 1 — Understand Why You Were Suspended

Twitter sends an email to your registered address when suspending an account. Check your email for details about the specific policy violated. Common reasons include:

  • Abusive behavior or harassment
  • Spam: mass posting, following/unfollowing rapidly, or automated behavior
  • Platform manipulation (buying followers, using bots)
  • Impersonating a person or organization
  • Sharing content that violates Twitter Rules
  • Suspicious login activity triggering security suspension

How to Appeal a Twitter Suspension

Method 1 — Appeal from the Suspension Banner

When you try to log in, Twitter shows a suspension notice with an Appeal button. Click it and complete the appeal form. This is the fastest route.

Method 2 — Appeal via Help Center

Visit help.twitter.com/forms/general → Select "I think my account was incorrectly suspended" → Enter your @username and email → Describe why you believe the suspension is in error. Be specific and professional.

Step 3 — Wait for Response

Twitter reviews appeals via email. Response time is typically 3–14 days. Check the inbox for the email associated with your Twitter account, including spam folders.

Step 4 — Submit a Follow-up

If you don't receive a response within 2 weeks, submit another request via help.twitter.com. Reference your original ticket number if you received one. Escalating politely can help move the queue.

If Your Account Was Locked (Not Suspended)

Verify Your Phone Number

Twitter locks accounts when it detects unusual activity. Log in → click "Start" → Enter your phone number → Enter the SMS verification code. Your account will unlock immediately.

Verify via Email

If you can't verify by phone, Twitter may offer email verification as an alternative. Check your registered email for a verification link from Twitter.

Tips for a Successful Appeal

  • Be polite and factual — aggressive appeals are less likely to succeed
  • Acknowledge any missteps and explain what happened in context
  • If the suspension was a mistake (e.g., false report by others), state that clearly
  • Provide your account age, typical usage, and follower count to establish legitimacy
  • Do NOT create a new account while appealing — this can result in the new account being suspended too

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Twitter while my appeal is being reviewed?

No. A suspended account cannot post, like, or access content while the suspension is active. You can only access the appeal/login screen.
