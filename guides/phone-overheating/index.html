Phone Overheating? Here's Why and How to Fix It

A warm phone during heavy use is normal — modern processors generate significant heat. But if your phone burns to the touch, shuts itself down, or throttles noticeably, that's a problem that needs attention. Here's everything you need to know.

1. Common Causes of Phone Overheating

🎮
Gaming / Streaming
3D games and 4K video push CPU and GPU to near maximum, generating heat rapidly.
🔋
Fast Charging
Fast and wireless charging produces more heat than standard 5W charging.
☀️
Direct Sunlight
Leaving your phone in sunlight or a hot car is the fastest path to thermal shutdown.
📱
Background Apps
Runaway background processes or location services can push CPU usage to 100%.
📶
Poor Signal
Phones work harder to maintain a signal in low-coverage areas, consuming more power.
🦠
Malware
Crypto-mining or adware malware can cause persistent overheating with no obvious cause.

2. Immediate Cool-Down Steps

If your phone is hot right now:

  1. Stop charging it — unplug the cable immediately if it's plugged in.
  2. Close all apps — double-tap the home button (iPhone) or use the recent apps button (Android) and swipe all apps away.
  3. Remove the case — thick cases trap heat. Let the phone breathe.
  4. Turn off the screen and set it on a flat hard surface — not on a bed or couch (fabric traps heat).
  5. Do NOT put it in the freezer — rapid temperature change causes condensation damage.
  6. Wait 5–10 minutes until it returns to normal temperature.
Warning: If your phone displays a temperature warning and shuts down, let it cool completely before turning it back on.

3. iPhone Overheating Fixes

Settings & Software

  1. Update iOS – Settings → General → Software Update. Apple regularly fixes thermal management bugs.
  2. Disable Background App Refresh – Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Off.
  3. Turn off Location Services for unused apps – Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → review each app, set non-essential apps to "Never."
  4. Disable 5G if unnecessary – Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data Options → Voice & Data → LTE. 5G modems run hotter than LTE.
  5. Check which apps drain battery – Settings → Battery → see usage over 24 hours. An app using unusual battery is likely causing heat too.
  6. Restart your iPhone – a simple restart clears rogue processes.

4. Android Overheating Fixes

Settings & Software

  1. Check battery usage – Settings → Battery → Battery Usage → identify any app using excessive battery.
  2. Disable location for unused apps – Settings → Location → App Permissions → review each app.
  3. Disable 5G – Settings → Connections → Mobile Networks → Network Mode → LTE/4G only.
  4. Update the phone – Settings → Software Update → Download and Install.
  5. Clear app cache for problematic apps – Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Storage → Clear Cache.
  6. Run a safe mode boot – hold Power → long-press "Power Off" → boot into safe mode. If the phone stays cool, a third-party app is the culprit.

5. Overheating While Charging

It's normal for a phone to get slightly warm while charging. Excessive heat during charging is a red flag:

  • Use the original charger and cable — third-party chargers sometimes exceed safe voltage limits.
  • Remove the case while charging if using fast charge or wireless charge.
  • Don't use the phone intensively while it's charging at the same time.
  • Charge in a cool, ventilated area — not under a pillow or on a carpet.
  • If the battery bulges or the phone gets very hot near the bottom, it may have a failing battery. Visit an authorized repair center.
Tip: Most modern iPhones and Android flagships support "optimized charging" — enable it to stop charging at 80% overnight and reduce heat buildup.

6. Overheating During Gaming

Mobile gaming is the most demanding use-case for phone cooling. Tips to reduce gaming heat:

  • Lower graphics settings in the game — reduce resolution or frame rate from 120fps to 60fps.
  • Enable the game's "performance mode" or "stability mode" if available (limits peak performance to reduce heat).
  • Take 10-minute breaks every hour of gaming.
  • Play with the phone in landscape on a table — airflow is better than holding it in both hands.
  • Consider a phone cooler accessory (Peltier-based fan mounts) for extended gaming sessions.

7. When Should You Be Worried?

Seek repair or replacement if:

  • The phone overheats even during light use (web browsing, texting).
  • The battery drains significantly faster than normal alongside overheating — signs of battery degradation.
  • You notice the back cover or battery area bulging.
  • The phone shuts down frequently due to temperature even in normal ambient conditions.
  • The phone was recently dropped in water or exposed to direct sunlight for extended periods.

All of these scenarios warrant a visit to an Apple Authorized Service Provider or a certified Android repair shop. A battery replacement often resolves chronic overheating in older phones.

9. Safety Warnings

⚠️ Battery Safety:
  • Never puncture or attempt to open a swollen battery — risk of fire or chemical burns
  • Do not attempt to replace phone batteries yourself unless you have proper tools and training
  • Dispose of damaged batteries at certified e-waste recycling centers
  • If battery shows signs of swelling (phone feels puffy, screen lifting), stop using immediately
