TikTok Account Banned? Here's What to Do

A TikTok account ban can be temporary (suspension) or permanent. Permanent bans are issued for serious or repeated Community Guidelines violations. You can appeal directly through the app — here's how, and what to expect.
Temporary vs Permanent: A suspension has a countdown timer and lifts automatically. A permanent ban says your account was "permanently banned" with no timer — you must appeal this type.

How to Appeal a TikTok Ban

Step 1 — Tap "Appeal" on the Ban Notice

When you try to log in, TikTok shows a ban notice. Tap the Appeal button directly on this screen. Do not ignore this screen — it's the primary appeals entry point.

Step 2 — Submit Your Appeal Form

TikTok will ask you to confirm your identity and describe why you believe the ban is incorrect. Be clear and specific: explain what your account was used for, why you believe no violation occurred, and any context that supports your case.

Step 3 — Wait for Review

TikTok's trust and safety team typically responds within 5–7 business days. You'll receive notification via email or the TikTok app if your account is reinstated or the ban upheld.

Step 4 — Submit a Second Appeal

If your first appeal is denied, you can submit a second appeal. Go to your account screen → Submit feedback → Account issue. Provide additional evidence or a more detailed explanation. A fresh reviewer may reach a different conclusion.

Step 5 — Contact TikTok Support via the Web

Visit support.tiktok.com → Submit a request. Select "Account Issue" and explain your situation. Include your username and the email address associated with the account.

Common Reasons TikTok Bans Accounts

Repeated Community Guidelines Violations

TikTok uses a strike system. Multiple strikes (for spam, unsafe content, harassment, etc.) result in permanent ban. Review the Community Guidelines in the app to understand what triggered the strikes.

Using Third-Party Automation Tools

Bots, auto-likers, auto-followers, and follower-buying services violate TikTok's Terms of Service. These tools are easily detected and result in account termination.

Age-Related Detection

TikTok bans accounts when it detects the user may be under 13 (per COPPA regulations). If wrongly flagged, provide verification of your age in the appeal.

Intellectual Property Violations

Repeatedly posting copyrighted music, videos, or content without permission leads to copyright strikes and potential account termination.

If Your Appeal Fails

If TikTok upholds the permanent ban and you've exhausted appeals, your options are limited. You can try the TikTok feedback form one more time at tiktok.com/legal/report/feedback or accept the ban and create a new account using different credentials, a different device, and a fresh phone number.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a TikTok suspension last?

Suspensions range from 24 hours to 30 days depending on the violation severity. Your ban notice will show a countdown timer for temporary suspensions.
