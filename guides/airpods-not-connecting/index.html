AirPods Not Connecting? Here's How to Fix It

AirPods connection issues are frustrating but usually fixable in under 5 minutes. This guide covers all the common causes and step-by-step solutions for AirPods not pairing with iPhone, iPad, Mac, or other devices.
Quick fix: Put your AirPods back in the case, wait 15 seconds, then open the lid near your iPhone. If no popup appears, proceed with the steps below.

Common Causes

  • AirPods battery is too low to pair
  • Bluetooth is off or glitching on your device
  • AirPods are already connected to another device
  • iOS/macOS needs an update
  • Corrupted pairing data
  • Dirty charging case contacts

Step-by-Step Fixes

Step 1 — Charge Your AirPods

Place both AirPods in the case and charge for at least 10 minutes. Low battery (below 10%) can prevent pairing entirely.

Step 2 — Toggle Bluetooth Off and On

On your iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth. Toggle it off, wait 5 seconds, then toggle back on. Open the AirPod case near your phone.

Step 3 — Forget and Re-Pair

Go to Settings → Bluetooth, tap the ⓘ icon next to your AirPods, tap Forget This Device. Then put AirPods in case, open lid near iPhone, and follow the pairing prompt.

Step 4 — Reset Your AirPods

Put AirPods in the case with lid open. Hold the button on the back of the case for 15 seconds until the LED flashes amber, then white. Now pair fresh.

Step 5 — Restart Your iPhone

A simple restart clears Bluetooth cache issues. Hold Side + Volume Down → slide to power off → restart.

Step 6 — Update iOS/macOS

Go to Settings → General → Software Update. AirPods firmware updates are delivered through iOS; keeping iOS current ensures the latest AirPods fixes.

Step 7 — Clean the Case Contacts

Use a dry cotton swab to gently clean the metal charging contacts inside the case and on each AirPod. Debris can disrupt charging and pairing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my AirPods connect?

Most pairing failures are caused by low battery, Bluetooth glitches, or the AirPods being paired to another device. A reset almost always resolves persistent issues.

How do I reset AirPods?

Place AirPods in the case, open the lid, and hold the setup button on the back for 15 seconds until the indicator light flashes amber then white. Then pair again.

Why do my AirPods keep connecting to the wrong device?

AirPods use iCloud to switch automatically between Apple devices. To prevent this, go to Bluetooth settings on the unwanted device and select "Connect to This Device: When Last Connected Here."
