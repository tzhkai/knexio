WhatsApp Web lets you use WhatsApp on your computer through a browser. When it stops working — whether it's refusing to connect, looping QR codes, or failing to send messages — these fixes will get you back up and running.

Keep Phone Connected: The Essential Requirement

Understanding WhatsApp Web's phone connection requirements is crucial before troubleshooting:

How WhatsApp Web Connection Works

WhatsApp Web operates as a companion to your phone, not a standalone service. Here's what you need to know:

  • Initial setup always requires your phone: To link WhatsApp Web, your phone must be connected to the internet to scan the QR code
  • Ongoing connection depends on your phone: Traditional WhatsApp Web requires your phone to maintain an active internet connection
  • Multi-device mode changes things: With WhatsApp's multi-device feature, linked devices can work independently for up to 14 days without the phone
  • After 14 days offline: If your phone hasn't connected to the internet in 14 days, WhatsApp Web will disconnect and require re-linking
Critical: Even with multi-device mode enabled, your phone must have internet access (WiFi or cellular data) when initially linking WhatsApp Web. The QR code cannot be scanned with a phone that has no internet connection.

When Your Phone Must Stay Connected

The phone must be connected to the internet (not just turned on) in these situations:

  • When first linking WhatsApp Web via QR code scan
  • When syncing new chats or contacts to WhatsApp Web
  • When receiving media files (photos, videos, documents)
  • If you haven't used WhatsApp on your phone in over 14 days
  • After updating WhatsApp on your phone (re-link may be required)
Pro Tip: Keep WhatsApp running in the background on your phone. On Android, lock it in the recent apps list. On iPhone, enable "Keep WhatsApp Running" in your device settings. This prevents the system from killing the app and disconnecting WhatsApp Web.

QR Code Scanning: Detailed Guide

The QR code on WhatsApp Web expires every 20 seconds and must be scanned within that window. Here's how to do it correctly:

Step-by-Step QR Code Scanning

  1. On your computer, go to web.whatsapp.com
  2. On your phone, open WhatsApp
  3. iPhone: Tap Settings (gear icon) → Linked Devices → Link a Device
  4. Android: Tap the three dots menu → Linked Devices → Link a Device
  5. Your phone camera will open — point it at the QR code on your computer screen
  6. Hold steady for 1-2 seconds until you see a confirmation message
  7. The WhatsApp Web page will automatically refresh and load your chats

QR Code Scanning Tips for Success

  • Increase screen brightness: Your phone camera needs good contrast to read the QR code. Set your computer screen to maximum brightness.
  • Zoom in on the QR code: Press Ctrl+Plus (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Plus (Mac) to zoom in. A larger QR code is easier to scan.
  • Reduce screen glare: Move away from windows or bright lights that cause reflections on your monitor.
  • Clean your phone camera: A smudged or dirty camera lens will struggle to focus on the QR code. Wipe it gently with a soft cloth.
  • Hold at the right distance: Position your phone about 6-8 inches from the screen. Too close or too far can prevent scanning.
  • Stay still: Keep your phone steady for a moment after pointing it at the code. Movement causes blur.
  • Remove phone case: Some thick cases or lenses can interfere with the camera. Try without it if scanning fails.

If QR Code Won't Scan or Keeps Refreshing

  • The QR code refreshes every 20 seconds — if it changes before you scan, simply wait for the next one
  • Clear any screen protector smudges or fingerprints on your phone camera
  • Try a different browser if one browser consistently fails
  • Update your phone's WhatsApp to the latest version
  • Update your phone's operating system
  • Restart both your phone and computer
  • Try the WhatsApp Desktop app instead of the browser version
Tip: If you're using a phone with a cracked camera lens or autofocus issues, the WhatsApp Desktop app for Windows or Mac might be a better option. It uses a different linking mechanism and doesn't require camera scanning.

Browser Compatibility Guide

WhatsApp Web officially supports these browsers. Using a supported and updated browser is essential for the best experience.

Google Chrome Recommended for best compatibility
Mozilla Firefox Full support
Apple Safari Full support (Mac only)
Microsoft Edge Full support
Opera Full support

Chrome (Recommended)

  1. Ensure Chrome is updated: Menu → Help → About Google Chrome
  2. Open Chrome settings → More Tools → Extensions → disable all extensions temporarily
  3. Try an Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N) — if it works there, an extension is causing the problem
  4. Clear cookies for web.whatsapp.com: Lock icon → Cookies → web.whatsapp.com → Remove
  5. Allow camera and microphone: Menu → Settings → Privacy and Security → Site Settings → Camera/Microphone → ensure web.whatsapp.com is allowed

Firefox

  1. Update Firefox: Menu → Help → About Firefox
  2. Clear cookies and cache: Menu → Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
  3. Check permissions: Click the lock icon in address bar → Permissions → ensure Camera and Notifications are allowed
  4. Disable extensions temporarily to test

Safari (Mac)

  1. Enable camera access: Safari → Settings (or Preferences) → Websites → Camera → Allow for web.whatsapp.com
  2. Enable notifications: Safari → Settings → Websites → Notifications → Allow for web.whatsapp.com
  3. Disable "Prevent cross-site tracking": This can interfere with WhatsApp Web's connection. Safari → Settings → Privacy → uncheck if needed.
  4. Clear history and cookies: Safari → Clear History → select "all history"

Microsoft Edge

  1. Update Edge: Menu → Help and Feedback → About Microsoft Edge
  2. Clear cookies: Menu → Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies → See all cookies → Remove for web.whatsapp.com
  3. Check site permissions: Menu → Site Permissions → Camera/Microphone → ensure web.whatsapp.com is allowed
  4. Try Edge InPrivate mode to test if extensions are causing issues
Browser Requirements: WhatsApp Web requires JavaScript to be enabled, cookies to be allowed, and camera access to be permitted. Private/incognito browsing may have restricted permissions.

Fix: WhatsApp Web Not Connecting

If WhatsApp Web shows "Connecting..." indefinitely or keeps disconnecting:

Connection troubleshooting

  1. Make sure your phone has an active internet connection (check WhatsApp on your phone).
  2. Refresh the WhatsApp Web page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R) and scan the QR code again.
  3. Try a different browser — Chrome is the most compatible with WhatsApp Web.
  4. Disable any VPN on your computer — VPNs can interfere with WhatsApp Web's WebSocket connection.
  5. Disable browser extensions, especially ad blockers (uBlock, AdGuard, privacy extensions) that may block connections.
  6. Check if WhatsApp servers are having issues at isitdownrightnow.com.

Network-Specific Troubleshooting

  • Corporate/School networks: Firewalls may block WebSocket connections. Try on a different network (e.g., phone hotspot).
  • VPN interference: Some VPNs block WhatsApp's servers. Try disabling your VPN temporarily.
  • Proxy servers: If you're behind a proxy, configure it in browser settings or try without it.
  • Firewall blocks: Some firewalls block web.whatsapp.com. Contact your network administrator.
Tip: WhatsApp Web uses WebSocket connections. If you're on a corporate or school network, these connections may be firewalled. Try on a different network (e.g., phone hotspot) to confirm.

Phone Connection Troubleshooting

If WhatsApp Web frequently disconnects or shows "Phone Disconnected", your phone's connection is the issue:

Check Phone Internet Connection

  • Verify your phone has an active internet connection (not just cellular signal)
  • Open any app (browser, YouTube) to confirm data is working
  • If on WiFi, ensure the connection is stable — try switching to cellular data
  • If on cellular, ensure you have an active data plan with data remaining

Fix Background App Issues on Android

  1. Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization → find WhatsApp → set to "Don't optimize"
  2. Settings → Apps → WhatsApp → Battery → Allow background activity
  3. Settings → Battery → Adaptive Battery → turn OFF (this restricts app background activity)
  4. Settings → Apps → WhatsApp → Mobile Data → ensure background data is allowed
  5. Lock WhatsApp in your recent apps list (swipe from recent apps and pin it)

Fix Background App Issues on iPhone

  1. Settings → WhatsApp → Background App Refresh → enable
  2. Settings → General → Background App Refresh → enable (set to Wi-Fi and Cellular)
  3. Settings → WhatsApp → ensure Cellular Data is enabled
  4. Do not force-quit WhatsApp — it needs to run in the background for WhatsApp Web to stay connected
  5. Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode → turn OFF (this disables background refresh)

Battery Saver Mode Conflicts

  • Android: Some phones have aggressive battery savers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei) that kill background apps. Add WhatsApp to the "Never sleeping apps" list in your battery settings.
  • iPhone: Low Power Mode significantly reduces background activity. Keep it off when using WhatsApp Web actively.
  • Third-party battery savers: If you use a battery-saving app, add WhatsApp to its exception list.

Fix: WhatsApp Web Messages Not Sending

Fix messages stuck or not delivering

  1. Check the icon next to sent messages:
    • Single gray tick: message sent from your phone
    • Double gray tick: delivered to recipient's device
    • Double blue tick: read by recipient
    • Clock icon: message is waiting to send (likely no connection)
  2. If messages show a clock icon, check your phone and computer internet connection
  3. Reload WhatsApp Web
  4. Make sure the recipient hasn't blocked you — if so, you'll only see one tick
  5. Check if your phone's WhatsApp is actually connected (look for the green "WhatsApp" status in the app)

Fix: WhatsApp Web Notifications Not Working

Enable browser notifications

  1. Click the lock/info icon in your browser's address bar next to web.whatsapp.com
  2. Find Notifications and set it to Allow
  3. If you don't see the lock icon, go to browser settings → Site Settings → Notifications → add web.whatsapp.com to the Allow list
  4. Make sure your computer's Do Not Disturb / Focus mode is not blocking notifications
  5. On Windows: Settings → Notifications → make sure your browser is allowed to send notifications
  6. On Mac: System Settings → Notifications → find your browser and enable Allow Notifications

Browser-Specific Notification Settings

  • Chrome: chrome://settings/content/notifications → add web.whatsapp.com
  • Firefox: Menu → Settings → Privacy & Security → Permissions → Notifications → Settings → add web.whatsapp.com
  • Safari: Safari → Settings → Websites → Notifications → Allow for web.whatsapp.com
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Notifications → add web.whatsapp.com
