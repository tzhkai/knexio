Spotify Playlist Not Syncing?

Fix Spotify sync issues across devices

Ad space

1. Check Your Internet Connection

Spotify requires internet to sync playlists. Make sure you're connected to WiFi or mobile data on all devices.

2. Log Out and Log Back In

Settings → Log Out on all devices. Then log back in. This refreshes your account and often fixes sync issues.

3. Force Sync

Open the playlist on each device and play a song. This forces Spotify to sync the playlist data.

4. Update Spotify

Make sure Spotify is updated to the latest version on all devices. Old versions may have sync bugs.

5. Clear Cache

Settings → Storage → Clear Cache. This removes cached data and can fix sync problems.
