Battery Draining Fast? Here's How to Fix It

Rapid battery drain is one of the most common smartphone and laptop complaints. The good news: most causes are software-related and can be fixed without replacing the battery. Here's a complete guide.
Check first: Go to Settings → Battery (iPhone) or Settings → Battery → Battery Usage (Android) to identify which apps are consuming the most power.

Top Causes of Fast Battery Drain

  • Screen brightness set too high
  • Background App Refresh enabled for all apps
  • Location services always on for multiple apps
  • Push notifications from dozens of apps
  • Outdated operating system with known battery bugs
  • Poor cellular signal causing the radio to work harder
  • Degraded battery (below 80% capacity)
  • A rogue app stuck in a loop

Fixes for iPhone

Step 1 — Enable Low Power Mode

Go to Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode. This reduces background activity, mail fetch, and visual effects. Saves up to 30% battery.

Step 2 — Reduce Screen Brightness

Swipe down Control Center and lower brightness. Also enable Auto-Brightness in Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size.

Step 3 — Limit Background App Refresh

Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Off or limit to Wi-Fi only.

Step 4 — Check Location Services

Settings → Privacy → Location Services. Set most apps to "While Using" instead of "Always."

Step 5 — Check Battery Health

Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging. If below 80%, consider a battery replacement at Apple Store.

Fixes for Android

Step 1 — Enable Battery Saver

Go to Settings → Battery → Battery Saver. This restricts background processes and reduces performance to extend life.

Step 2 — Identify Draining Apps

Settings → Battery → Battery Usage. Force-stop any app using unusually high battery percentage.

Step 3 — Disable Always-On Location

Settings → Location → App Permissions. Change apps from "Always allow" to "Allow only while using."

Step 4 — Update Android & Apps

Many battery drain issues are fixed in software updates. Check Settings → System → Software Update.

Fixes for Laptops (Windows/Mac)

Windows — Enable Battery Saver

Click the battery icon in the system tray → Enable Battery Saver. Or go to Settings → System → Power & Sleep → Battery Saver.

Mac — Check Battery Usage

Click the battery menu bar icon → "Battery Preferences" → see which apps are "Using Significant Energy." Quit those apps when not needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my phone battery draining so fast?

Screen brightness, background apps, location services, and push notifications are the top culprits. Check battery usage in Settings to identify the biggest consumers.

What drains battery overnight?

Push email, background app refresh, and weak cellular signals are common overnight drain causes. Enable Low Power/Battery Saver mode before bed.
