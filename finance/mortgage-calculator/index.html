Free Online Mortgage Calculator

Our mortgage calculator helps you estimate your monthly mortgage payments, including principal, interest, property taxes, insurance, and PMI. Use it to plan your home purchase and understand your total housing costs. Calculate monthly payments, total interest, and view a complete amortization schedule showing how each payment is applied to principal and interest over time.

How It Works

Enter your home price and down payment to determine the loan amount. Add your interest rate, loan term, and optional costs like property taxes, insurance, and PMI. Click Calculate to see a full breakdown including an amortization schedule. The calculator uses the standard amortization formula to calculate your monthly principal and interest payment, then adds monthly property tax, insurance, and PMI to determine your total monthly payment.

The amortization schedule shows each payment over the life of your loan, breaking down how much goes toward principal and interest. You can see how your loan balance decreases over time and how much interest you'll pay in total. This helps you understand the true cost of your mortgage and plan your finances accordingly.

Our calculator includes support for PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance), which is typically required if your down payment is less than 20% of the home price. You can also enter property tax and insurance amounts to get a complete picture of your monthly housing costs. The calculator works for both fixed-rate mortgages and can help you compare different loan terms to find the best option for your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mortgage calculator?

A mortgage calculator is a tool that helps you estimate your monthly mortgage payments based on loan amount, interest rate, loan term, and additional costs like taxes and insurance.

How do I use this mortgage calculator?

Enter the home price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, and optional costs like property tax, insurance, and PMI. Click Calculate to see your monthly payment breakdown and amortization schedule.

What is PMI and when is it required?

PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is typically required if your down payment is less than 20% of the home price. It protects the lender in case you default on the loan and usually costs 0.5-2% of the loan amount annually.

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule shows each payment over the life of your loan, breaking down how much goes toward principal and interest. It helps you see how your loan balance decreases over time.

How accurate are mortgage calculator estimates?

Mortgage calculator estimates are accurate for standard fixed-rate mortgages. However, actual payments may vary due to factors like rate changes, additional fees, or changes in property taxes and insurance.

Is this mortgage calculator free to use?

Yes, our mortgage calculator is completely free to use with no registration required. Calculate unlimited scenarios to help you plan your home purchase.
