FAQ

What cryptocurrencies does this calculator support?

Any cryptocurrency works with this calculator. Enter the coin name (e.g. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana), the amount you bought, your purchase price, and the current price — the calculator handles any coin or token.

Does this calculator account for fees?

No — enter your net purchase price (after fees) for the most accurate results. Trading fees vary by exchange, so including them in your purchase price gives you a true profit/loss figure.

How do I calculate my average purchase price for multiple buys?

For multiple purchases at different prices, calculate your average: (amount1 × price1 + amount2 × price2) ÷ (amount1 + amount2). Enter that average price as your purchase price.

Is cryptocurrency profit taxable?

In most countries (including the US), cryptocurrency profits are taxable as capital gains. This calculator is for informational purposes only — consult a tax professional for your specific obligations.

Does this work for any cryptocurrency?

Yes, works for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and all altcoins.
