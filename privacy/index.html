Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected about them
  • Request deletion of their personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of their personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising their CCPA rights

Knexio does not sell personal information. However, third-party services (Google AdSense, Google Analytics) may use cookies and data for advertising purposes. To opt out of the "sale" of personal information as defined by the CCPA, you can:

  • Enable "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" via our cookie banner (select "Decline")
  • Visit Google Ads Settings to opt out of personalized ads
  • Use the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page
