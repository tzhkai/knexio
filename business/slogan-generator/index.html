What Makes a Great Slogan?

Effective slogans share common characteristics that make them memorable:

  • Memorable: Easy to remember and repeat.
  • Short: Typically 3-7 words for maximum impact.
  • Unique: Distinguishes your brand from competitors.
  • Relevant: Reflects your brand's values and offerings.
