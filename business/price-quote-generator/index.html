How to Use

  1. Enter client name and quote number — Identify your quote for easy tracking
  2. Add your line items — List each product or service on a new line (e.g. Web Design — $2,500)
  3. Set the tax rate — Enter your applicable tax percentage, or leave at 0 for no tax
  4. Set validity date — Define when this quote expires to create urgency
  5. Click Generate — A professional, itemized price quote appears instantly
  6. Copy and send — Copy the formatted quote and email it to your client

Features

  • Unlimited line items — Add as many products or services as you need per quote
  • Automatic totals — Subtotal, tax, and grand total calculated instantly
  • Customizable tax rate — Set any percentage, including 0 for tax-exempt quotes
  • Quote validity date — Add expiration dates to create natural follow-up opportunities
  • One-click copy — Copy the entire formatted quote in one click, ready to paste into email
  • 100% free — No sign-up, no limits, no watermarks
