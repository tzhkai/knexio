FAQ

What is competitor analysis?

Competitor analysis is the process of evaluating a competitor's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) to inform your own business strategy and positioning.

When should I conduct a competitor analysis?

Do a competitor analysis before launching a new product, entering a new market, planning a marketing campaign, or annually as part of your strategic review.

How often should I update my competitor analysis?

Review and update your competitor analysis at least every 3–6 months. Markets shift quickly — new competitors emerge, pricing changes, and product features evolve.

How do I find data for competitor analysis?

Use public sources: competitor websites, LinkedIn, Glassdoor reviews, social media, Crunchbase, SimilarWeb traffic estimates, and G2 for SaaS products.

What's the difference between SWOT and Porter's Five Forces?

SWOT focuses on your own company vs. competitors (internal + external). Porter's Five Forces analyzes industry-level forces like supplier power, buyer power, and threat of substitution — broader than a single competitor comparison.

Can I use this for academic or consulting purposes?

Yes. The SWOT framework generated here is a standard business tool suitable for academic reports, consulting deliverables, and strategic planning documents.
