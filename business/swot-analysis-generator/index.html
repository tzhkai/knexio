Understanding SWOT Analysis

SWOT analysis is a strategic planning technique used to identify:

  • Strengths: Internal characteristics that give your business an advantage.
  • Weaknesses: Internal characteristics that place your business at a disadvantage.
  • Opportunities: External factors that your business could exploit to its advantage.
  • Threats: External factors that could cause trouble for your business.

When to Use SWOT Analysis

SWOT analysis is valuable for business planning, competitive analysis, strategic decision-making, and identifying new opportunities. Use our generator to create comprehensive SWOT analyses quickly.
