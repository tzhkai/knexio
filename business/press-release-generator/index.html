Press Release Best Practices

Writing an effective press release requires following certain conventions:

  • Strong Headline: Capture attention with a clear, compelling headline.
  • Newsworthy Content: Focus on what makes your announcement important.
  • Inverted Pyramid: Put the most important information first.
  • Quote: Include a quote from a relevant spokesperson.
  • Contact Info: Always include media contact information.
