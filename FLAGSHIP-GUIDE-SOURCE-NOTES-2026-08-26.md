## Editorial rules for implementation

1. The article will identify an example as a **public-source walkthrough** when it depends on the linked external material. Any invented record will be labeled **illustrative composite** and **not a client case**.
2. Sources will sit in a visible “Sources & method” module. Each citation will name the publisher, title, access role, and link rather than hiding a source in the prompt.
3. The method text will distinguish: what the source documents, what the article’s workflow asks a reader to do, and what a reviewer must still verify.
4. No source will be used to make a claim that it does not substantiate. Updates to external datasets will be described by the source's stated update behavior, not represented as a fixed result.
