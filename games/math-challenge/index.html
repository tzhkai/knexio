Game Description

Math Challenge is an educational game that tests your arithmetic skills. Solve math problems quickly and accurately to score points and improve your mental math!

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Solve the math problem displayed on screen
  2. Type your answer and press Enter
  3. Faster correct answers earn more points
  4. Wrong answers reduce your score

Pro Tips
