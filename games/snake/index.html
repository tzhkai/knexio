🎮 How to Play Snake

Game Description

Snake is a classic arcade game where you control a growing snake to collect food while avoiding collisions with walls and your own tail.

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Use arrow keys (↑, ↓, ←, →) to control the snake's direction
  2. Move the snake to eat the food (red dot) that appears randomly
  3. Each piece of food makes the snake grow longer
  4. Avoid hitting the walls or the snake's own body
  5. The game gets progressively harder as the snake grows longer
  6. Your score increases with each piece of food collected

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Plan your path before making turns
  • ✓ Try to keep the snake in open areas
  • ✓ Use the walls to your advantage for tight turns
  • ✓ Don't get trapped in corners
  • ✓ Stay calm as the snake gets longer
