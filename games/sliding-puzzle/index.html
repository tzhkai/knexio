How to Play

The goal is to arrange the numbered tiles in ascending order by sliding them into the empty space.

  • Click or tap a tile adjacent to the empty space to move it
  • Arrange tiles in order from 1 to 15 (or 8 for 3×3)
  • The empty space should end up in the bottom-right corner
  • Try to solve it in as few moves as possible!

About Sliding Puzzle

The 15-puzzle (also called Gem Puzzle, Boss Puzzle, or Mystic Square) is a classic sliding puzzle that became popular in the 1880s. It consists of a frame of numbered square tiles in random order with one tile missing.
