🎮 How to Play Doodle Jump

Game Description

Doodle Jump is an addictive platform-jumping game. Bounce higher and higher on platforms, avoid obstacles and enemies, and see how high you can climb!

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Tilt or use arrow keys to move left/right
  2. Jump on platforms to keep climbing upward
  3. Avoid or shoot enemies and broken platforms
  4. Fall off the bottom and it's game over!

Pro Tips

  • Aim for springs and trampolines for super jumps
  • Shoot enemies by tapping/clicking
  • Stay centered to react quickly to both sides
