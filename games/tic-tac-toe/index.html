🎮 How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Game Description

Tic Tac Toe is the classic strategy game. Get three of your marks in a row to win. Simple to learn, but requires strategy to master!

How to Play

  1. Click a square to place your mark (X or O)
  2. Get 3 in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win
  3. Take turns with your opponent
  4. Block your opponent's winning moves
  5. Game is a draw if all squares fill with no winner
  6. Play against AI or challenge a friend

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Take the center square first for the best advantage
  • ✓ Create a fork - two ways to win at once
  • ✓ Block opponent's forks while creating your own
  • ✓ Corners are better than edges for opening moves
