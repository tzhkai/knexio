🎮 How to Play Reaction Speed

Game Description

Reaction Speed tests how fast you can respond to visual cues. Wait for the signal, then click as quickly as possible. Measure and improve your reaction time!

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Wait for the color change or visual signal
  2. Click as fast as possible when it appears
  3. Your reaction time is measured in milliseconds
  4. Try to beat your personal best!

Pro Tips

  • Stay focused but relaxed
  • Don't click early—anticipation hurts your score
  • Practice improves average reaction time
