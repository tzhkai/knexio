How to Play Solitaire

Objective

Move all cards to the four foundation piles (top right), building up in suit from Ace to King.

The Tableau (Main Play Area)

  • Build columns down in alternating colors (red on black, black on red)
  • Only Kings can be placed on empty tableau spots
  • Face-down cards are revealed when the card on top is moved

The Stock and Waste Piles (Top Left)

  • Click the stock pile to draw cards
  • Draw 1: One card at a time
  • Draw 3: Three cards at a time (harder)
  • Top card of waste pile can be played

The Foundation Piles (Top Right)

  • Build up in suit from Ace to King
  • Once placed, cards can be moved back to tableau if needed

Scoring

  • Move to foundation: +10 points
  • Move tableau to tableau: +5 points
  • Turn over face-down card: +5 points
  • Recycle stock pile: -100 points (Draw 3 only)
