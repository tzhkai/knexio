🎮 How to Play Maze Runner

Game Description

Maze Runner is a puzzle navigation game. Find your way from start to finish through complex mazes. Race against the clock or take your time!

How to Play

  1. Arrow keys or swipe to move through the maze
  2. Find the path from the green start to red finish
  3. Avoid dead ends - they waste time
  4. Complete the maze as fast as possible
  5. Mazes get larger and more complex with each level
  6. Some mazes have multiple possible routes

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Follow the left-hand or right-hand rule for guaranteed exit
  • ✓ Look ahead and plan your route when possible
  • ✓ Don't panic if you hit a dead end - backtrack calmly
  • ✓ Memorize patterns for similar maze structures
