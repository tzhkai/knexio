🎮 How to Play Flappy Bird

Game Description

Flappy Bird is an addictive arcade game where you control a bird flying through pipes. Tap to make the bird flap and navigate through gaps between pipes without hitting them.

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Click/tap anywhere on the screen to make the bird flap upward
  2. Each tap gives the bird a small upward boost
  3. Release to let gravity pull the bird downward
  4. Navigate through the gaps between pipes
  5. Each pipe passed gives you 1 point
  6. The game ends if the bird hits a pipe or the ground

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Tap lightly and rhythmically rather than frantically
  • ✓ Try to maintain a consistent altitude
  • ✓ Time your flaps to pass through the center of pipe gaps
  • ✓ Stay calm - panicking leads to over-tapping
  • ✓ Practice consistency over speed
