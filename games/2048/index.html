🎮 How to Play 2048

Game Description

2048 is a classic number puzzle game where you slide tiles on a 4×4 grid to combine them and reach the 2048 tile. Every move creates new tiles with values of 2 or 4, and when two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value.

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Use arrow keys (↑, ↓, ←, →) or swipe on touch devices to move all tiles
  2. Tiles slide as far as possible in the chosen direction until they hit the wall or another tile
  3. When two tiles with the same number collide while moving, they merge into one tile with their sum
  4. After each move, a new tile with value 2 (90% chance) or 4 (10% chance) appears in a random empty spot
  5. The game ends when no more moves are possible (grid is full with no possible merges)
  6. Your score increases by the value of merged tiles (e.g., merging two 8s gives 16 points)

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Keep your highest-value tile in a corner (usually bottom-right)
  • ✓ Build chains of increasing numbers in one direction
  • ✓ Try to avoid moving tiles in the opposite direction of your main strategy
  • ✓ Plan several moves ahead to avoid getting stuck
  • ✓ Use the 'Undo' button to recover from mistakes
