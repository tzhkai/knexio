🎮 How to Play Connect Four

Game Description

Connect Four is a classic strategy game. Drop colored discs into a grid and be the first to connect four in a row - vertically, horizontally, or diagonally!

How to Play

  1. Click a column to drop your disc into the lowest empty space
  2. Take turns with your opponent
  3. Connect 4 discs in a row to win
  4. Connections can be vertical, horizontal, or diagonal
  5. Plan ahead - block opponent's winning moves
  6. Game is a draw if the board fills with no winner

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Control the center columns - they offer more winning possibilities
  • ✓ Watch for '7' trap setups that create two winning threats
  • ✓ Block opponent's three-in-a-row immediately
  • ✓ Think two moves ahead - set up your own double threats
