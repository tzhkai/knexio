How to Play

Objective

Be the first to connect 4 of your discs in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Taking a Turn

Click (or tap) any column to drop your disc. It falls to the lowest available row in that column.

AI Difficulty

Easy: random moves. Medium: blocks obvious wins. Hard: uses minimax with alpha-beta pruning — very tough to beat!

Draw

If the entire 6×7 board fills up with no winner, the game is a draw. Scores track across games.
