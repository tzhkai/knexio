🎮 How to Play Asteroids

Game Description

Asteroids is a classic arcade space shooter. Navigate your spaceship through an asteroid field, destroying space rocks while avoiding collisions. The more asteroids you destroy, the higher your score!

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Use arrow keys or WASD to rotate and thrust your ship
  2. Press SPACE to fire bullets and destroy asteroids
  3. Large asteroids break into smaller ones when hit
  4. Avoid crashing into asteroids—each collision costs a life

Pro Tips

  • Keep moving to avoid being an easy target
  • Clear smaller asteroids first—they're faster and harder to hit
  • Use momentum to drift and shoot in different directions
