🎮 How to Play Asteroids

Game Description

Asteroids is a classic arcade shooter. Pilot your spaceship through an asteroid field, destroy rocks to score points, and avoid collisions to survive!

How to Play

  1. Arrow keys or WASD to rotate and thrust
  2. Spacebar to shoot lasers
  3. Destroy asteroids to earn points
  4. Large asteroids break into smaller ones when hit
  5. Avoid collisions - your ship has limited lives
  6. Hyperspace (if available) teleports you to safety

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Stay near the center to have room to maneuver
  • ✓ Destroy large asteroids early before they multiply
  • ✓ Use momentum to drift while shooting
  • ✓ Don't stay in one place - keep moving!
