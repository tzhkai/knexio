🎮 How to Play Traffic Racer

Game Description

Traffic Racer is an endless driving game. Speed down a highway, weave through traffic, and avoid crashes. How far can you go without an accident?

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Use arrow keys or swipe to change lanes
  2. Avoid other vehicles on the road
  3. Collect coins or power-ups for bonuses
  4. Speed increases over time—stay alert!

Pro Tips

  • Stay in lanes with fewer cars
  • Look ahead to anticipate traffic patterns
  • Don't make sudden lane changes at high speed
