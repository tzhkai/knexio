How to Play Gomoku

Gomoku, also known as Five in a Row, is a classic strategy board game that originated in Japan.

  • Players take turns placing black and white stones on the board intersections
  • Black always goes first
  • The first player to get exactly 5 stones in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins
  • More than 5 in a row does not count as a win in standard rules

Strategy Tips

  • Control the center of the board
  • Create multiple threats (forks) to force a win
  • Block your opponent's open-ended lines of 3 or 4
  • Build open-ended lines (spaces on both ends) for flexibility

🎮 How to Play Gomoku

Game Description

Gomoku (Five in a Row) is an ancient strategy game. Place stones on a grid and be the first to connect five in a row!

How to Play

  1. Click an intersection to place your stone
  2. Black plays first, then players alternate
  3. Connect 5 stones in a row to win
  4. Connections can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal
  5. Overlines (6+ stones) may or may not count depending on rules
  6. Block opponent's four-in-a-row threats

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Control the center of the board for maximum flexibility
  • ✓ Create multiple threats simultaneously (forks)
  • ✓ Watch for open threes - they can become unstoppable fours
  • ✓ Defense is crucial - one missed block can end the game
