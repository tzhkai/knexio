How to Play Gomoku

Gomoku, also known as Five in a Row, is a classic strategy board game that originated in Japan.

  • Players take turns placing black and white stones on the board intersections
  • Black always goes first
  • The first player to get exactly 5 stones in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins
  • More than 5 in a row does not count as a win in standard rules

Strategy Tips

  • Control the center of the board
  • Create multiple threats (forks) to force a win
  • Block your opponent's open-ended lines of 3 or 4
  • Build open-ended lines (spaces on both ends) for flexibility

🎮 How to Play Gomoku

Game Description

Gomoku (Five in a Row) is an ancient strategy board game played on a 15×15 grid. Be the first to place 5 stones in a row!

Game Rules

  • Black plays first, then players alternate
  • Click any empty intersection to place your stone
  • Stones cannot be moved once placed
  • Win by connecting exactly 5 stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally
  • More than 5 in a row does not count as a win

Strategy Tips

  • Control the center of the board for more winning lines
  • Create "open fours" (4 in a row with both ends open) to guarantee a win
  • Block opponent's potential fives while building your own
  • Look for double threats - two potential winning lines at once
