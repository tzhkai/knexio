🎮 How to Play Word Scramble

Game Description

Word Scramble challenges you to unscramble letters to form words. Rearrange the scrambled letters to find the hidden word!

How to Play

  1. Look at the scrambled letters provided
  2. Rearrange them to form a valid word
  3. Type or click letters in the correct order
  4. Use hints if you get stuck (may cost points)
  5. Solve before time runs out for maximum points
  6. Multiple difficulty levels with longer words

Pro Tips & Strategies

  • ✓ Look for common letter combinations (TH, SH, CH, etc.)
  • ✓ Try placing vowels in likely positions
  • ✓ Say possible words out loud - hearing helps recognition
  • ✓ Start with shorter words to build confidence
