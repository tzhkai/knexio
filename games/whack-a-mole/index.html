🎮 How to Play Whack A Mole

Game Description

Whack-a-Mole is a fast-paced reflex game. Moles pop up from holes—tap them before they disappear! Score as many points as you can before time runs out.

Basic Rules & Controls

  1. Click or tap moles when they appear
  2. Faster reactions earn more points
  3. Moles disappear quickly—be fast!
  4. Score as many hits as possible in the time limit

Pro Tips

  • Watch all holes, not just one area
  • Use two hands on mobile for faster coverage
  • Anticipate patterns in mole appearance
